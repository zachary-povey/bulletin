## Top stories

### Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang; Megaspeed blacklisted; Congress to question him

Nvidia's board voted unanimously on Monday 23 November to bring Jensen Huang back as president, chief executive and director. He had resigned on 12 November after reports of extramarital affairs, one with a former executive of a Nvidia customer whom US intelligence reportedly suspects of working for China. The reversal moved markets sharply and has set up a confrontation with lawmakers of both parties.

**Established facts**

- Nvidia said the board acted after "input from senior levels of the United States government" and is "confident that Mr. Huang's reinstatement poses no national security risk". Its independent investigation continues, and a Chief Compliance Officer is to be hired. Colette Kress, the interim chief executive, remains chief financial officer. [Nvidia](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director), [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/23/nx-s1-6050910/nvidias-jensen-huang-trump)
- Nvidia shares rose about 31% on Monday, adding almost $1tn in market value, then closed nearly 6% lower on Tuesday 24 November. [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cq5y1k8ez407o), [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/crpdw1qx7jgxo)
- The US Commerce Department added Megaspeed, a Singapore cloud provider, and affiliates (seven businesses in all, including an Alibaba subsidiary) to its Entity List in a rule filed on Monday. [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/systems/2026/11/24/us-blacklists-megaspeed-hours-after-jensen-huang-returns-to-nvidia/5311901), [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/crpdw1qx7jgxo)
- Attorney General Todd Blanche declined on Tuesday to say whether Huang or Nvidia had been investigated, calling Nvidia "a good corporate citizen". [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/crpdw1qx7jgxo), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/24/todd-blanche-declines-to-confirm-if-nvidias-jensen-huang-was-investigated)
- House Democrats wrote to Trump on 24 November objecting to his involvement. Republicans including Don Bacon and Pete Ricketts also criticised the decision. [House Energy and Commerce Democrats (PDF)](https://democrats-energycommerce.house.gov/sites/evo-subsites/democrats-energycommerce.house.gov/files/evo-media-document/2026.11.24-multi-committee-letter-to-president-trump-re-jensen-huang.pdf), [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/crpdw1qx7jgxo)

**Claimed or disputed**

- Trump says he "made the deal" and that federal officials "exonerated" Huang. Huang reportedly told staff on Tuesday that Trump's direct involvement was "completely untrue". Blanche would not confirm any investigation had taken place. [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/ckj05m2zdyryo), [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1041571/jensen-huang-all-hands-nvidia-apology), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/24/todd-blanche-declines-to-confirm-if-nvidias-jensen-huang-was-investigated)
- The Financial Times, citing unnamed people, reports that Trump spoke to the board himself, that Vice-President Vance held a 45-minute call with Huang, and that Kress was kept out of the talks. Nvidia declined to comment. [FT via Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/11/how-trump-and-silicon-valley-forced-nvidia-to-take-jensen-huang-back/)
- The espionage allegation rests on anonymous intelligence sourcing. Beijing calls it a smear, and Megaspeed says its business is lawful. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/24/todd-blanche-declines-to-confirm-if-nvidias-jensen-huang-was-investigated), [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/systems/2026/11/24/us-blacklists-megaspeed-hours-after-jensen-huang-returns-to-nvidia/5311901)

**Opinion and analysis**

- Bernstein analysts called the board's on-again, off-again response "clown-like". [FT via Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/11/how-trump-and-silicon-valley-forced-nvidia-to-take-jensen-huang-back/)
- Edward Fishman of the Council on Foreign Relations told *Wired* that some companies are now so important to the stock market that Washington is reluctant to hold them accountable. [Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/jensen-huang-nvidia-us-national-security-risks/)
- The BBC's economics editor Faisal Islam argues fears about the AI boom "have not gone away", noting Anthropic is trading notably below last week's flotation level. [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c6894evgpel5o)

**What next**: Huang is due before three congressional committees on Tuesday 1 December, and Nvidia's general counsel the following day.

### 1Password discloses breach of backend systems

1Password said on Wednesday 25 November that an attacker used an employee's corporate GitHub account to steal source code and push malicious code to backend services, copying account records for a subset of customers. The company says vault contents and keys were not accessible.

**Established facts**

- 1Password began investigating on 19 November. Data taken includes names, email addresses, account identifiers and tokens from some invitation and recovery emails. The attacker also reached its CI/CD system. [1Password](https://1password.com/blog/update-on-nov-2026-security-incident), [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1042409/1password-data-breach-leak)
- The company has not said how many customers are affected. It did not answer *The Register*'s questions about how a GitHub account allowed code to be deployed to its backend. [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/11/26/hackers-help-themselves-to-1passwords-source-code-and-customer-data/5312317)

**Claimed or disputed**

- 1Password says vaults were never available to the attacker, that apps and browser extensions were unaffected, and that it found no account takeovers. These are the company's statements and have not been independently verified. [1Password](https://1password.com/blog/update-on-nov-2026-security-incident)

**What next**: 1Password says it will update its post if findings change. It warns customers to expect phishing.

### FCC approves SpaceX plan for up to one million data-centre satellites

The US Federal Communications Commission on Wednesday 25 November approved SpaceX's Starmind constellation, intended to run AI computing in low-Earth orbit, the first approval of its kind.

**Established facts**

- FCC chairman Brendan Carr said the project "opens a bold new frontier". The FCC deferred SpaceX's request to dispose of satellites above 600km in "graveyard" orbits. [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cwp836l8n1jgo), [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/science/1042278/spacex-fcc-starmind-ai-space-data-centers)

**Claimed or disputed**

- The two outlets give different deployment milestones: the BBC says deployment must begin by November 2033, with 10% by 2035 and 50% by 2038; The Verge says one satellite by 2033 and the full million by 2040. The FCC order itself was not opened. [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cwp836l8n1jgo), [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/science/1042278/spacex-fcc-starmind-ai-space-data-centers)
- Amazon called the plan a "lofty ambition rather than a real plan"; US astronomers said it would "significantly undermine" study of the night sky. [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cwp836l8n1jgo)
