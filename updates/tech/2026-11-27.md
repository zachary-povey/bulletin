## Top stories

### Nvidia reportedly pauses server shipments to south-east Asia

The Financial Times and the Wall Street Journal both report that Nvidia has told manufacturing partners to stop shipping servers to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia while it checks whether buyers there meet new US export requirements. It is the first commercial fallout reported from the rules, which Washington tightened during the turmoil over Jensen Huang's departure and return. Nvidia has not confirmed the pause.

**Established facts**

- New US controls took effect on 19 November. They require a US licence to ship advanced AI chips to Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Brazil, and are aimed at Chinese firms renting computing power in data centres abroad. [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/nvidia-halts-server-shipments-southeast-asia), [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqevr4lnd1nxo)
- Tech-heavy stock indexes in Japan, South Korea and Taiwan swung sharply this week, first on Nvidia's rebound and then on worries about political and regulatory scrutiny. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqevr4lnd1nxo)

**Claimed or disputed**

- According to the FT, which cites three people familiar with the matter, Dell and Supermicro told buyers in the four countries that their orders would not ship. The paper says it is unclear whether the halt is temporary. The WSJ, as relayed by TechCrunch, describes it as a pause "for an unspecified period". Neither original article could be opened. Nvidia, Dell and Supermicro did not respond to requests for comment. [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/nvidia-halts-server-shipments-southeast-asia), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/26/nvidia-hits-pause-on-chip-shipments-to-southeast-asia/), [FT](https://www.ft.com/content/7838a715-07d4-4605-aa60-21f5af2b2370) (feed summary only)
- The WSJ cites an estimate from the Institute for Progress, a think tank, that overseas data centres like these supply about 24% of China's AI computing power. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/26/nvidia-hits-pause-on-chip-shipments-to-southeast-asia/)
- The claim that Alice Huang, the former Megaspeed executive, is a Chinese spy remains unproven. Reports conflict on whether the classified State Department memo actually concluded that she was one: the WSJ says it did not, CNN says it did. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/26/is-alice-huang-a-spy-or-not/)

**Opinion and analysis**

- Charu Chanana of Saxo Bank told the BBC that Huang's return "does not resolve the legal and political overhangs". Dylan Loh of Nanyang Technological University called Nvidia a "symbolic bellwether" for the region's AI economies. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqevr4lnd1nxo)
- In the *Guardian*, the entrepreneur Anil Dash says Huang's reinstatement means "we're all being gaslit". Supporters such as Jason Calacanis frame the return as necessary to beat China in AI. [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/26/jensen-huang-nvidia-tech)

**What next**: Huang is due to testify before Congress next week.

### Bank of England: frontier AI is making cyber risk a bigger threat to stability

On Thursday 26 November the Bank of England's Financial Policy Committee published its Financial Stability Report. It says frontier AI capabilities, "notably in the sophistication of AI agents and open-weight AI models", are making cyber and operational risks a more prominent threat to financial stability. The same report cuts bank capital requirements.

**Established facts**

- The report describes AI-related financing as "increasingly complex, circular and opaque" and says hyperscaler bond issuance is set to exceed $305bn in 2026, around 8% of global investment-grade issuance. [Bank of England](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/financial-stability-report/2026/november-2026)
- It says open-weight models, whose safeguards are "more easily disabled or removed", can make advanced AI more useful to malicious actors. It also urges firms to speed up preparations for quantum-resistant cryptography. [Bank of England](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/financial-stability-report/2026/november-2026)
- Tier 1 capital requirements for major UK banks will fall by the equivalent of about 50 basis points of risk-weighted assets. [Bank of England](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/financial-stability-report/2026/november-2026)

**Claimed or disputed**

- The FT reports that Governor Andrew Bailey said unreleased AI models had raised encryption concerns. The article could not be opened, and the report itself does not say this. [FT](https://www.ft.com/content/450920b9-82b8-4a66-960b-5740aa7f94ae) (feed summary only)

### SAP reportedly weighs at least 10,000 job cuts as it shifts to AI

Three outlets, each citing its own unnamed sources, report that SAP is considering cutting more than 10,000 jobs as part of a restructuring around AI. That would be roughly 9% of its staff.

**Established facts**

- SAP said AI is "rapidly changing our business environment", that no decisions on further measures had been taken, and that it does not comment on speculation. It employed about 113,000 people at the end of the third quarter. [dpa-AFX via finanzen.at](https://www.finanzen.at/nachrichten/aktien/roundup-3-kreise-sap-erwagt-abbau-wegen-ki-von-rund-10-prozent-aller-stellen-1036803968)

**Claimed or disputed**

- Handelsblatt, citing company sources, and Bloomberg, as relayed by dpa-AFX, both report cuts of more than 10,000. Bloomberg says the number could still change in talks with unions. The FT reported the same figure, but its article could not be opened. [Handelsblatt](https://handelsblatt.com/technik/ki/sap-dax-konzern-plant-massiven-stellenabbau/100273115.html), [dpa-AFX via finanzen.at](https://www.finanzen.at/nachrichten/aktien/roundup-3-kreise-sap-erwagt-abbau-wegen-ki-von-rund-10-prozent-aller-stellen-1036803968), [FT](https://www.ft.com/content/ef05b4ab-7944-44ae-ad00-15475f8c1ec9) (feed summary only)

**Opinion and analysis**

- JPMorgan analyst Toby Ogg estimates that the cuts could add €750m to adjusted operating profit, with the full effect from 2028. [dpa-AFX via finanzen.at](https://www.finanzen.at/nachrichten/aktien/roundup-3-kreise-sap-erwagt-abbau-wegen-ki-von-rund-10-prozent-aller-stellen-1036803968)

**What next**: SAP has usually announced restructurings with its annual results, which are due at the end of January.

### Microsoft says Chinese and North Korean state hackers use the ARTEX AI framework

Microsoft Threat Intelligence says ARTEX, an open source AI penetration-testing framework released in July, has been adopted by state-backed groups including Salt Typhoon, Crystal Typhoon, Onyx Sleet and Pearl Sleet, as well as by criminals.

**Established facts**

- Microsoft says that, depending on its configuration and the target's defences, ARTEX can go from reconnaissance to persistent privileged access "in a matter of minutes". It uses only known techniques, and can run on any model, including models whose guardrails have been removed. [Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/11/25/what-defenders-need-to-know-artex-agentic-ai-framework-threat-actors-abuse/), [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/11/26/microsoft-hackers-in-china-n-korea-using-artex-ai-tool-to-break-into-global-tech-systems/5312433)
- China's ThreatBook claimed ownership of ARTEX in November and now sells "ARTEX Pro". [Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/11/25/what-defenders-need-to-know-artex-agentic-ai-framework-threat-actors-abuse/)
- The UK's National Cyber Security Centre reports a "marked rise" in AI-assisted attacks since September. It notes that these tools are noisy and therefore easier to detect. [NCSC](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/blogs/familiar-weaknesses-exploited-faster-defending-against-agentic-cyber-attacks)

**Claimed or disputed**

- The Register cites Infrawatch's figure of more than 130 ARTEX variants in circulation. This has not been verified. [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/11/26/microsoft-hackers-in-china-n-korea-using-artex-ai-tool-to-break-into-global-tech-systems/5312433)

### UK watchdog says Meta declined joint testing of its Muse agent

Colin Payne, head of innovation at the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA), told *Wired* that Meta turned down a proposal to test its Muse personal agent in the regulator's sandbox before the agent's UK launch on Tuesday 24 November. The Competition and Markets Authority (CMA) and the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) also warned Meta about compliance.

**Established facts**

- The CMA has made Meta change its UK terms so that Meta must exercise "reasonable skill and care" when providing Muse. Meta says it has "engaged constructively" with regulators (single source). [Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/meta-refused-to-share-muse-for-testing-uk-financial-watchdog-complains/)
