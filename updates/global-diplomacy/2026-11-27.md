## Top stories

### EU budget summit ends in walkout and no deal

The extraordinary summit that opened in Brussels on Thursday 26 November ended early on Friday without agreement on the 2028-2034 budget, which is tied to a new €45bn loan to Ukraine for 2027. Leaders now aim for a deal at the 17 December summit, before France's presidential election in April.

**Established facts**

- Denmark, Sweden, Belgium and Austria walked out of António Costa's small-group sessions. Austria said in a statement that further sessions "would not bring us closer to a solution". Costa cut his 1am press conference to 11 minutes and confirmed that leaders "didn't reach an agreement" (single source). [Politico Europe](https://www.politico.eu/article/eu-budget-summit-walkout-antonio-costa-leaves-one-month-negotiations-drama/)
- Costa's proposal of about €1.78tn is a 10% cut on the Commission's €2tn plan. Leaders are split between net contributors who want a smaller budget focused on defence and 17 southern, central and eastern states defending farm and regional funds. [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/26/eu-leaders-braced-for-clashes-over-ukraine-aid-at-budget-talks), [Politico Europe](https://www.politico.eu/article/eu-budget-summit-walkout-antonio-costa-leaves-one-month-negotiations-drama/)
- On the Ukraine loan, Friedrich Merz repeated that the EU means "27 member states, not 23 or 24". Bulgaria's Rumen Radev said Sofia would join the €20bn non-military part but give "no money ... for weapons". Al Jazeera reports that Costa said early on Friday that 24 member states backed the loan. [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/26/eu-leaders-braced-for-clashes-over-ukraine-aid-at-budget-talks), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/27/zelenskyy-warns-of-major-russian-attack-as-trump-urges-putin-to-pull-back)
- Giorgia Meloni said "we're light-years away from a solution". Hungary's Peter Magyar wrote that a Christmas deal "does not seem likely either". [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/11/26/eu-leaders-struggle-to-agree-on-long-term-budget-more-money-for-ukraine)

**Claimed or disputed**

- EU diplomats quoted by Politico say Costa plans a new proposal cutting a further €220-230bn, mainly from competitiveness and foreign aid. This has not been announced. [Politico Europe](https://www.politico.eu/article/eu-budget-summit-walkout-antonio-costa-leaves-one-month-negotiations-drama/)
- The Guardian reports that Germany wants future EU funding decisions for Kyiv to be taken by majority vote rather than unanimity, to protect them from a possible Marine Le Pen presidency. Le Pen called the push to settle the budget before the French election "underhanded". [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/26/eu-leaders-braced-for-clashes-over-ukraine-aid-at-budget-talks), [France 24](https://www.france24.com/en/le-pen-slams-push-for-eu-budget-deal-before-french-election)

**Opinion and analysis**

- Emmanuel Macron called Costa's cuts "a strategic mistake" and said talks running into 2027 were "not democratically possible". One unnamed EU diplomat called him "Monsieur Chantage" (Mr Blackmail). Meloni said: "I don't have a deadline, I have conditions." [Politico Europe](https://www.politico.eu/article/eu-budget-summit-walkout-antonio-costa-leaves-one-month-negotiations-drama/)
- Politico reports that capitals are now more willing to confront Washington over a proposed EU digital levy and "European preference" in defence procurement. The US State Department said it opposes the levy; Luxembourg's Luc Frieden and Sweden voiced concerns. [Politico Europe](https://www.politico.eu/article/eu-leaders-brave-clash-with-trump-over-tech-tax-defense-ukraine/)

**What next**: The European Council meets on 17 December, a summit diplomats expect to last several days.

### Trump rewrites joint NATO statement to warn Ukraine as well as Russia

**Established facts**

- After their meeting at Mar-a-Lago on Wednesday 25 November, the joint statement published by NATO stressed "the importance of Russia avoiding any actions that intentionally, or unintentionally, expand the war". [NATO](https://www.nato.int/en/news-and-events/articles/news/2026/11/26/joint-statement-by-the-president-of-the-united-states-of-america-and-the-nato-secretary-general)
- Hours later Trump posted a version on Truth Social that read "Russia and Ukraine". A NATO official told Politico that the original text was the one both sides had agreed. The White House did not comment. [Politico Europe](https://www.politico.eu/article/donald-trump-diverges-from-nato-statement-emphasizing-ukraine-mustnt-expand-war/), [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/trump-adds-ukraine-to-warning-against-expanding-war-diverging-from-joint-nato-statement/)
- In a separate post, Trump said he had urged Putin in China to "pull back all Military aggression against territories throughout Europe" and called a peace deal "a strong possibility". [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/27/zelenskyy-warns-of-major-russian-attack-as-trump-urges-putin-to-pull-back), [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c820q9wgrpyyo)
- Russia and Ukraine exchanged war dead on 26 November: 250 bodies went to Ukraine and 22 to Russia, according to a Russian lawmaker. Peskov said lists for a large prisoner swap were being finalised (single source). [Moscow Times](https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2026/11/26/russia-and-ukraine-hold-first-exchange-of-war-dead-since-rebooted-peace-talks-a812378)

**Claimed or disputed**

- In a BBC interview, Zelensky said the US was delaying Patriot interceptor deliveries because of its focus on the Middle East, and that it was "strange" for Trump to call Putin a friend. He said talks are expected to resume in December, but it is unclear whether Europeans will take part. He also said Russia is preparing a new mass strike. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c820q9wgrpyyo), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/27/zelenskyy-warns-of-major-russian-attack-as-trump-urges-putin-to-pull-back)
- Ukraine's military intelligence (GUR) says North Korea has paused deliveries of KN-23 missiles and shells to Russia after a diplomatic dispute. It also says Pyongyang is holding off on a Russian request to raise its troop presence from about 8,500 to 50,000. Neither Moscow nor Pyongyang has confirmed this. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/27/zelenskyy-warns-of-major-russian-attack-as-trump-urges-putin-to-pull-back), [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/ukraine-war-latest-north-korea-halts-military-aid-to-russia-ukrainian-intelligence-says/)

**Opinion and analysis**

- Politico's Victor Jack writes that the edit shows the limits of Rutte's strategy of keeping Trump on message through praise. Zelensky defended the strike on the corvette Merkury off Libya as an "absolutely fair goal". [Politico Europe](https://www.politico.eu/article/donald-trump-diverges-from-nato-statement-emphasizing-ukraine-mustnt-expand-war/), [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c820q9wgrpyyo)

**What next**: NATO foreign ministers meet in Brussels on 2 December. The US has told allies that its review of US forces in Europe will be delayed.

### Armenia moves further from Moscow ahead of Pashinyan's visit

**Established facts**

- On 26 November Nikol Pashinyan said Armenia would take "full responsibility" for its borders "in the coming years", ending the role of Russian FSB guards on its borders with Turkey and Iran that dates from a 1992 agreement. He gave no timeline. [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/armenia-plans-full-withdrawal-of-russian-border-guards-in-coming-years-prime-minister-says/), [Moscow Times](https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2026/11/26/pm-pashinyan-reaffirms-armenia-s-eu-ambitions-ahead-of-putin-meeting-a812383)
- He said repealing Armenia's EU accession law was not "possible", and that he hopes his 3 December visit to Moscow will ease tensions. The Eurasian Economic Union is expected to discuss suspending Armenia's membership in December (single source). [Moscow Times](https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2026/11/26/pm-pashinyan-reaffirms-armenia-s-eu-ambitions-ahead-of-putin-meeting-a812383)

**What next**: Pashinyan meets Putin on 3 December.

### New Zealand's ruling coalition loses its majority

**Established facts**

- Official results released on Friday 27 November leave Christopher Luxon's National-ACT-NZ First bloc one seat short of a majority (65 of 130 seats). It needs the seven-seat Opportunity Party, which says "all options remain on the table". A Labour-led government is not arithmetically possible, and a fresh election is possible if talks fail (single source for seat detail). [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c670278qk879o)
