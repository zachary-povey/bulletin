## Top stories

### US proposes "excess capacity" tariffs on 14 trading partners

On Wednesday 25 November the US Trade Representative proposed new tariffs on China, the EU, India, Japan and ten other economies, a week after the Trump-Xi meeting at APEC and weeks before the US-China truce expires on 10 January. The duties are not final, and outlets report the rates differently.

**Established facts**

- The proposal follows an eight-month Section 301 investigation into "structural excess capacity", opened after the Supreme Court struck down Trump's emergency-powers tariffs in February. It covers China, the EU, India, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan, Vietnam, Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand, Cambodia, Bangladesh, Singapore and Switzerland; Mexico and Norway were investigated but not included. [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c893r9ke5p97o), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/25/trump-announces-new-trade-tariffs-which-countries-will-they-target)
- Written comments are due by 5 January 2027 and hearings begin on 25 January (single source). [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/business/Economy/experts-say-new-us-tariffs-do-not-pose-substantial-threat-india-should-not-rush-into-trade-deal/article71746007.ece)
- Separately, Washington proposed a 10% tariff on Brazilian finished aircraft and higher duties on sugar; President Lula's office said "Brazil will not yield to such threats". [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c893r9ke5p97o), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/11/25/world-reacts-to-trumps-new-tariffs-as-us-stocks-tumble)
- The European Commission said it "strongly reject[s] the premise that Europe is a source of structural overcapacity"; China's foreign ministry called overcapacity "a false issue". [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/11/25/world-reacts-to-trumps-new-tariffs-as-us-stocks-tumble)

**Claimed or disputed**

- The rates are reported inconsistently. The BBC gives China "up to 40%", Vietnam up to 35% and others 20% or 25%; Al Jazeera, citing a US official quoted by Reuters, gives 20% for China on top of roughly 25% from Trump's first term; ABC gives a 45% total on some Chinese goods and 10-18% for most others; The Hindu reports a net 25% for India, falling to 17% with a trade deal. US officials say caps in existing deals (15% for the EU, Japan and South Korea) will be respected. [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c893r9ke5p97o), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/25/trump-announces-new-trade-tariffs-which-countries-will-they-target), [ABC](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-26/trump-new-tariffs-major-trading-partners/107425504), [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/business/Economy/experts-say-new-us-tariffs-do-not-pose-substantial-threat-india-should-not-rush-into-trade-deal/article71746007.ece)

**Opinion and analysis**

- Georgetown law professor Gregory Shaffer calls the use of Section 301 "cynical", designed to strip Congress of tariff authority. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/11/25/world-reacts-to-trumps-new-tariffs-as-us-stocks-tumble)
- Stephen Brown of Capital Economics says the move is "not as alarming as it looks" because it largely replaces earlier tariffs; trade analyst Dmitry Grozoubinski calls these Trump's "most legally robust" tariffs so far. [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c893r9ke5p97o), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/25/trump-announces-new-trade-tariffs-which-countries-will-they-target)

**What next**: Comment deadline 5 January; US-China truce expiry 10 January; the G20 summit in mid-December.

### Trump and NATO warn Russia against widening the war; Moscow blames Kyiv

After Russian missiles and drones entered Polish airspace on 23 and 24 November, and after Ukrainian strikes on Russian vessels off Finland and Libya, both sides' backers are warning against geographic escalation.

**Established facts**

- Trump and NATO chief Mark Rutte said in a joint statement on Wednesday 25 November that Russia should avoid actions that "intentionally, or unintentionally, expand the war". On Thursday Trump wrote that he had asked Putin to "pull back all Military aggression against territories throughout Europe". [Moscow Times](https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2026/11/26/trump-and-nato-chief-urge-russia-to-avoid-actions-that-widen-ukraine-war-a812374), [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/trump-tells-putin-to-pull-back-all-military-aggression-across-europe/)
- Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov replied that it was Kyiv that was expanding "the geographical scope of this conflict" in the Mediterranean and Baltic. [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/international/kremlin-accuses-ukraine-of-trying-to-widen-conflict-zone-to-mediterranean-and-baltic-seas/article71747857.ece), [Moscow Times](https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2026/11/26/trump-and-nato-chief-urge-russia-to-avoid-actions-that-widen-ukraine-war-a812374)
- Italy's Giorgia Meloni said the war "should not and cannot extend to the Mediterranean"; Germany's foreign minister Johann Wadephul also cautioned against geographic spread. Both reaffirmed support for Ukraine. [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/italy-and-germany-caution-against-geographical-expansion-of-russias-war-against-ukraine/), [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/international/kremlin-accuses-ukraine-of-trying-to-widen-conflict-zone-to-mediterranean-and-baltic-seas/article71747857.ece)
- Poland plans a no-fly zone along its border with Ukraine by early 2027 (single source). [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/poland-plans-no-fly-zone-near-ukraine-border-asks-nato-to-intercept-russian-missiles-over-ukraine/)
- Putin said on Tuesday 24 November that retaliation for Ukrainian strikes on refineries was "inevitable". [Moscow Times](https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2026/11/24/putin-says-ukraine-hits-russian-oil-plants-despite-trumps-requests-promises-retaliation-a94367)

**Claimed or disputed**

- Poland asked NATO ambassadors on 23 November to consider intercepting Russian missiles over Ukraine, according to Bloomberg's anonymous sources; a Polish deputy minister said NATO was not supportive. [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/poland-plans-no-fly-zone-near-ukraine-border-asks-nato-to-intercept-russian-missiles-over-ukraine/)
- US negotiators have privately ruled out troops or bases in post-war Ukraine, the Kyiv Independent reports from unnamed sources. The FT, as relayed by the Moscow Times, reports Washington has shelved business deals as an incentive for Moscow; Reuters could not verify it. [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/exclusive-us-rules-out-troops-military-bases-in-ukraine-under-post-war-security-guarantees-falling-short-of-kyivs-expectations/), [Moscow Times](https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2026/11/25/us-shelves-plans-for-business-deals-with-russia-to-end-ukraine-war-ft-a94370)

**Opinion and analysis**

- Elisabeth Braw of the Atlantic Council tells DW that Kyiv is showing it can hit Russian ships "wherever" they are; Joachim Weber of Bonn University warns of a rising risk of accidental clashes at sea. [DW](https://www.dw.com/en/russia-ukraine-war-extends-to-the-baltic-and-the-mediterranean/a-80054604)

**What next**: Peskov says lists for further prisoner exchanges are being finalised; no date is set for the next round of talks.

### EU leaders debate ending opt-outs on Ukraine funding

An EU summit opened in Brussels on Thursday 26 November with a proposed 45 billion euro loan to Kyiv for 2027 tied to the 2028-2034 budget. No outcome had been reported in the sources read.

**Established facts**

- Chancellor Friedrich Merz said all 27 states must finance Ukraine, "not 23 or 24"; Bulgaria, Czechia, Hungary and Slovakia want opt-outs. Czech prime minister Andrej Babis called the budget link "completely absurd" (single source). [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/solidarity-and-winter-top-eu-agenda-as-leaders-discuss-new-support-for-ukraine/)
- On Monday 23 November the Commission proposed a 22nd sanctions package: 60 more banks cut from SWIFT, a crypto task force, a visa ban on Russian ex-combatants and a ban on Russians competing under their flag in the EU (single source). [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/23/eu-plans-sports-ban-in-sweeping-new-russia-sanctions-proposal)

**What next**: Summit conclusions, and member-state approval of the sanctions package.

### US-backed consortium signs deal for Lukoil's foreign assets

**Established facts**

- A consortium led by billionaire Todd Boehly, including the US International Development Finance Corporation, announced on 25 November a share-swap deal for Lukoil's international arm. It needs approval from the US Treasury's OFAC. [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/us-consortium-announces-deal-to-acquire-sanctioned-russian-oil-company-lukoils-global-subsidiary/), [Moscow Times](https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2026/11/25/american-billionaire-signs-u-s-backed-share-swap-deal-to-buy-lukoil-global-a812372)

**Claimed or disputed**

- Boehly and a DFC official say the deal curbs Moscow's war funding; Democratic congressman Gregory Meeks says it shows the administration will "empower Russia and undercut Ukraine". [Moscow Times](https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2026/11/25/american-billionaire-signs-u-s-backed-share-swap-deal-to-buy-lukoil-global-a812372), [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/us-consortium-announces-deal-to-acquire-sanctioned-russian-oil-company-lukoils-global-subsidiary/)

### Myanmar says it will not fully comply with ICJ genocide ruling

**Established facts**

- Min Aung Hlaing said on Wednesday 25 November that Myanmar "would not comply with requirements it considered inappropriate" in last week's ICJ ruling that it committed genocide against the Rohingya, according to state media; he did not specify which. The UK has sanctioned senior military figures (single source). [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c61wr87wyg5wo)

**What next**: Enforcement would fall to the UN Security Council; reparations are still to be set.

### Kim Jong Un calls for more nuclear weapons after Trump's overture

**Established facts**

- North Korean state media said on Thursday 26 November that Kim inspected nuclear-material plants and called wider deployment of tactical nuclear weapons "urgent"; he said denying the country's nuclear status would be "an open declaration of war". No Trump-Kim meeting took place during Trump's APEC trip. [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/26/g-s1-155955/north-korea-kim-nuclear-weapons-trump), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/26/kim-jong-un-says-north-korea-should-widen-deployment-of-tactical-nukes)
