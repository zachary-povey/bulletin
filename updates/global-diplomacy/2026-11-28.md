## Top stories

### Kyiv and European lawmakers turn on Kushner over Putin praise

A podcast interview with Jared Kushner, Trump's peace envoy, released on Wednesday 25 November drew a wave of criticism on 26-27 November. It deepens the strain between Kyiv and Washington after Trump's edit to the joint NATO statement the same day.

**Established facts**

- Speaking on Axel Springer chief executive Mathias Döpfner's "MD Meets" podcast, Kushner called Putin "a grand master of politics" and "a historic figure". He said Putin "has shown restraint" on nuclear weapons, and described the war as having "effectively become a proxy war where Europe is funding and arming Ukraine against Russia". [Politico Europe](https://www.politico.eu/article/an-advocate-for-russia-jared-kushner-vladimir-putin-comments-spark-outrage-europe/), [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/nov/27/jared-kushner-interview-peril-putin-corporate-rival), [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/how-russia-reacted-kushner-praising-putin-political-grandmaster-12770391)
- Kushner said US envoys had raised an immediate ceasefire with Putin but concluded that he lacked confidence a halt would lead to a lasting settlement. He called the territorial question "a little bit of a deadlock" and proposed agreeing a European security framework first. [Kyiv Post](https://www.kyivpost.com/post/91223), [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/nov/27/jared-kushner-interview-peril-putin-corporate-rival)
- Oleksandr Merezhko, who chairs the Ukrainian parliament's foreign affairs committee, said Kushner and Steve Witkoff "need to be replaced". The White House did not respond to requests for comment. [Politico Europe](https://www.politico.eu/article/an-advocate-for-russia-jared-kushner-vladimir-putin-comments-spark-outrage-europe/)
- The Kremlin had not commented as of Thursday 26 November, but the state agency RIA ran his praise as a headline. [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/how-russia-reacted-kushner-praising-putin-political-grandmaster-12770391)

**Opinion and analysis**

- In the centre-left *Guardian*, diplomatic editor Patrick Wintour argues that the interview shows a "naive bias towards the aggressor", presented as a mediator's even-handedness. [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/nov/27/jared-kushner-interview-peril-putin-corporate-rival)
- Germany's Roderich Kiesewetter (CDU) said the US is "not acting as an honest broker". MEP Marie-Agnes Strack-Zimmermann called the proxy-war framing "pure Kremlin propaganda". Kushner's own case is that the aim is "not about fairness or who is right or wrong. It's about ending the killing." [Politico Europe](https://www.politico.eu/article/an-advocate-for-russia-jared-kushner-vladimir-putin-comments-spark-outrage-europe/), [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/nov/27/jared-kushner-interview-peril-putin-corporate-rival)

**What next**: Zelensky expects talks to resume in December. NATO foreign ministers meet on 2 December.

### Ukraine offers a Black Sea truce; Libya strike strains ties with Rome

**Established facts**

- On Friday 27 November, Foreign Minister Andrii Sybiha said Kyiv had passed Black Sea ceasefire proposals to allies and was "awaiting a response from Russia", thanking India, Turkey and Egypt for mediating. He said Moscow wants Ukraine to stop attacking Russia's oil sector, which Kyiv would consider only as part of a wider energy ceasefire (single source). [France 24](https://www.france24.com/en/europe/20261127-ukraine-submits-black-sea-ceasefire-offer-awaits-russian-response)
- Hours after Italian Foreign Minister Antonio Tajani said "what Zelensky said is not true", Zelensky said after a call with Giorgia Meloni that the 22 November strike on the corvette Merkury in Benghazi was "carried out without prior coordination with our European partners". [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/zelensky-says-ukraine-aligns-naval-strikes-with-partners-italy-denies-coordination-over-libya-attack/), [Politico Europe](https://www.politico.eu/article/ukraine-strikes-russian-ships-libya-giorgia-meloni-italy/)
- Putin commissioned the Yasen-M nuclear submarine Perm at Severodvinsk on 27 November and said five more submarines would join the fleet by 2030. [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/international/putin-unveils-new-nuclear-submarine/article71753555.ece), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/27/putin-unveils-new-russian-nuclear-powered-submarine-with-hypersonic-weapons)

**Claimed or disputed**

- Three military and security sources told Reuters that a Russian convoy of three sanctioned cargo ships carrying military vehicles to Libya turned back near the Strait of Sicily after the Benghazi strike. Russia's defence ministry did not comment. [Moscow Times](https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2026/11/27/russian-military-flotilla-bound-for-libya-turns-around-after-warship-sunk-a812400)
- Russia's foreign ministry accused Latvia of obstructing Russian grain transit and of letting Ukrainian drones cross its airspace, offering no evidence. Separately, Latvia said NATO jets were scrambled when a drone of unstated origin briefly entered its airspace overnight. [Moscow Times](https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2026/11/27/russia-accuses-latvia-of-obstructing-grain-transit-aiding-ukrainian-drone-a812399), [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/international/nato-jets-scrambled-after-drone-enters-latvian-airspace/article71752112.ece)

**What next**: Moscow's reply to the Black Sea offer. At APEC last week, Putin said Russia would no longer consider a Black Sea moratorium.

### Europe's money for Ukraine: Berlin pays, Sofia bargains

**Established facts**

- The Bundestag passed Germany's 2027 budget (€555.94bn in spending) on Friday 27 November. It includes €11.6bn for Ukraine, about half of the €25bn gap left by the planned €45bn EU loan. [Bundestag](https://www.bundestag.de/dokumente/textarchiv/2026/kw48-de-haushaltsgesetz-2027-dritte-lesung-1232350), [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/germany-votes-through-nearly-12-billion-euros-in-ukraine-support-for-2027/)
- After the failed EU budget summit, Merz said "hundreds of billions" still need to be cut. Slovakia's Robert Fico accused Germany of "blackmail" for tying the budget to the Ukraine loan. Macron said that after 31 December he could not "politically or democratically" reopen the talks. [DW](https://www.dw.com/en/divisions-deepen-between-eu-member-states-after-tense-budget-summit/a-80078599), [France 24](https://www.france24.com/en/europe/20261127-france-and-germany-push-for-eu-budget-deal-by-year-end-as-far-right-rise-threatens-ukraine-aid)

**Claimed or disputed**

- Diplomats told the Kyiv Independent that Bulgaria is demanding that EU bans on Russian pig iron and mining equipment be lifted, and that a chemicals firm and a nuclear-linked individual be dropped from the 22nd sanctions package, in return for letting the package pass. Italy also raised objections. Sofia has not commented publicly (single source). [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/bulgaria-holding-eus-22nd-russia-sanctions-package-hostage-in-bid-to-extract-concessions/)

**What next**: Costa is due to table a new budget proposal before the summit on 17-18 December.

### Uganda to pull troops from Lubero after DR Congo row

**Established facts**

- On Friday 27 November, Uganda's army said it would "soon" withdraw from Lubero in North Kivu. Earlier in the week army chief Muhoozi Kainerugaba had threatened on X to seize Walikale, and Kinshasa summoned Uganda's envoy over remarks it called "particularly serious" (single source for the withdrawal). [France 24](https://www.france24.com/en/africa/20261127-uganda-withdraw-troops-dr-congo-town-after-army-chief-threat-row)

**Claimed or disputed**

- The army spokesman accused militias, some apparently allied with Kinshasa, of massacring Hema and Tutsi civilians, and said Uganda retains a duty to protect them. Kinshasa has not responded publicly. [France 24](https://www.france24.com/en/africa/20261127-uganda-withdraw-troops-dr-congo-town-after-army-chief-threat-row)

### Costa Rica bars UN rapporteur Albanese, testing its UN chief candidate

**Established facts**

- On Thursday 26 November, Costa Rica refused entry to Francesca Albanese, UN special rapporteur on the occupied Palestinian territory. Its immigration chief cited an "international alert due to links to terrorist acts" but did not say which country issued it. UN rights chief Volker Türk urged an urgent reversal. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/27/costa-rica-denies-entry-to-uns-albanese-over-links-to-terrorist)
- More than 400 signatories urged Rebeca Grynspan, Costa Rica's candidate for UN secretary-general, who led the 20 November straw poll, to press her government to admit Albanese. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/28/un-chief-frontrunner-urged-to-speak-out-over-albanese-detention-2)

**What next**: Grynspan has not commented. Albanese said she would fly out on Saturday 28 November.
