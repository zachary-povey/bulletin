## Top stories

### Net migration falls to 77,000

ONS provisional estimates published on Thursday 26 November put net migration at 77,000 in the year to June 2026, down from 204,000 a year earlier. It is the first figure in the "tens of thousands" since David Cameron's 2010 pledge, and Labour and the Conservatives both claimed the credit.

**Established facts**

- Immigration was 738,000 and emigration 661,000; the ONS says this is the lowest net figure since 1997, excluding the pandemic. [ONS](https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/populationandmigration/internationalmigration/bulletins/longterminternationalmigrationprovisional/yearendingjune2026), [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq36gj12n0l3o)
- The fall was driven by non-EU arrivals: work down 45,000, study down 33,000, asylum down 19,000. Net migration of British nationals was minus 137,000 and of EU+ nationals minus 45,000. [ONS](https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/populationandmigration/internationalmigration/bulletins/longterminternationalmigrationprovisional/yearendingjune2026)
- The ONS cautions that the British-national estimates rest on an extended 15-month projection and are subject to greater revision. [ONS](https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/populationandmigration/internationalmigration/bulletins/longterminternationalmigrationprovisional/yearendingjune2026)

**Claimed or disputed**

- Home Secretary Shabana Mahmood said the government had "restored control"; shadow home secretary Chris Philp said the fall was "mainly a result of changes made by the previous Conservative government" and called for a legally binding cap. Reform UK's Zia Yusuf said the figures were "no cause for celebration". [Standard/PA](https://www.standard.co.uk/news/politics/ons-david-cameron-shabana-mahmood-chris-philp-office-for-national-statistics-b1306489.html), [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq36gj12n0l3o)

**Opinion and analysis**

- Ben Brindle of Oxford's Migration Observatory said the figure could fall further next year but "the downwards blip is unlikely to last forever". [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq36gj12n0l3o)
- Economist Jonathan Portes warned the push to lower immigration was "likely to be bad for growth, productivity and the public finances"; IPPR's Marley Morris said the government "must make sure it doesn't overreach". [Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/uk/politics/net-migration-figures-latest-ons-uk-b3092026.html)

### UK-EU treaty texts and implementing bill published

The government published two of the three treaty texts agreed at the 20 November summit, and introduced the bill to implement them on Thursday 26 November. Opposition parties say they would undo the deal; a tuition-fees agreement remains unresolved.

**Established facts**

- The 'Cutting Costs' (European Partnership) Bill implements the food (SPS) and emissions-trading agreements. Second reading is on 7 December, the first Commons vote on the deal. [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/cutting-costs-european-partnership-bill-frequently-asked-questions), [Standard/PA](https://www.standard.co.uk/news/politics/andy-burnham-kemi-badenoch-mps-prime-minister-commons-b1306510.html)
- The food deal has a 10-year termination clause under which a party ending it pays "reasonable and proportionate costs" of rebuilding border checks. The UK gets observer status but no vote on relevant EU rules, and the bill gives ministers Henry VIII powers to align laws (single source). [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c98r7p65edvgo)
- At PMQs on Wednesday 25 November Andy Burnham said EU students paying UK-level fees "won't be put on the table"; his spokesman declined to pre-empt the talks. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cw4gn8lxj5r1o), [Standard/PA](https://www.standard.co.uk/news/politics/andy-burnham-kemi-badenoch-mps-prime-minister-commons-b1306510.html)

**Claimed or disputed**

- Government modelling projects up to £5.1bn a year from the SPS deal and £3.8bn from ETS linking by 2040; the Conservatives say shoppers will save "a few pennies". These are forecasts. [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/cutting-costs-european-partnership-bill-frequently-asked-questions), [Standard/PA](https://www.standard.co.uk/news/politics/andy-burnham-parliament-kemi-badenoch-government-prime-minister-b1306454.html)
- Burnham told MPs the government had "removed the threat" from the EU's "Made in Europe" scheme; an Irish presidency draft that would admit the UK without matching tariffs on Chinese cars is still only a proposal. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c50md8x9gdgro)

**Opinion and analysis**

- BBC political editor Chris Mason questions whether Burnham can "turn the volume back down" on Europe having raised it himself. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6pw47jyyz5no)
- The left-leaning *New Statesman* argues that opposing the deal commits Kemi Badenoch to reimposing trade barriers; John Rentoul in the *Independent* highlights warnings from Lord Mandelson and former prime ministers about how hard rejoining would be. [New Statesman](https://www.newstatesman.com/politics/2026/11/pmqs-badenoch-gives-burnham-a-vibe-check), [Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/voices/eu-brexit-rejoin-reset-uk-mandelson-b3092037.html)

**What next**: Commons second reading on 7 December; tuition-fee talks are due to conclude within six months.

### Energy price cap to rise 26% in January

Ofgem announced on Wednesday 25 November that the cap for a typical dual-fuel household will rise from £1,723 to £2,166 a year from January, blaming wholesale gas prices driven by the Iran war.

**Established facts**

- The rise is £443 a year, the largest since Russia's invasion of Ukraine; the government will pay millions of households £100 from 4 January. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cm20qnq33e90o), [LBC](https://www.lbc.co.uk/article/no-10-energy-bill-ofgem-price-cap-5HjdnYy_2/)

**Claimed or disputed**

- Downing Street said Labour's 2024 pledge to cut bills by £300 "hasn't changed", although the typical bill will be £598 above its level when the pledge was made. [LBC](https://www.lbc.co.uk/article/no-10-energy-bill-ofgem-price-cap-5HjdnYy_2/)

### Timms review recommends tying Pip to extra costs

The government-commissioned review of personal independence payment, published on Tuesday 24 November, says the system is "no longer fit for purpose" and should reflect claimants' actual additional costs. It proposes no savings.

**Established facts**

- Ministers accepted fast-track awards for severe or end-of-life conditions and fewer reassessments; the full response comes early next year alongside the Milburn review. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cm4gn6gn9057o), [Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/uk/politics/pip-timms-review-welfare-disability-benefits-burnham-b3090622.html)
- The report says its approach implies a "rebalancing", meaning some claimants could receive less or not qualify. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cm4gn6gn9057o), [LBC](https://www.lbc.co.uk/article/disability-benefit-process-too-complex-dehumanising-5HjdnTG_2/)

**Claimed or disputed**

- Shadow work and pensions secretary Helen Whately said the review "tinkers around the edges"; Reform UK's Robert Jenrick said it would not cut the benefits bill. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cm4gn6gn9057o)

**Opinion and analysis**

- The IFS's Eduin Latimer said easier applications "would likely increase the number of claimants"; the Resolution Foundation also expects short-term costs. [Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/uk/politics/pip-timms-review-welfare-disability-benefits-burnham-b3090622.html)
