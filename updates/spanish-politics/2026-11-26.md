## Top stories

### Final four-way debate closes a campaign in which PP leads but PSOE has narrowed the gap

Pedro Sánchez (PSOE), Alberto Núñez Feijóo (PP), Santiago Abascal (Vox) and Ada Colau (Frente Amplio, the left-wing coalition of Sumar, IU, Más Madrid and Comuns) debate on Thursday 26 November at 22:00, the last set-piece before Sunday's vote. The debate had not taken place when this update was written. Polls can no longer be published; the last ones pointed to a PP–Vox majority.

**Established facts**

- The debate, organised by the Academia de Televisión and moderated by María Casado, has five 20-minute blocks (housing; tax and economy; social policy; corruption and democratic quality; migration and foreign policy). It is Feijóo's first debate against Abascal and Colau. [RTVE](https://www.rtve.es/noticias/20261126/feijoo-sanchez-abascal-colau-debate-cuatro/17331146.shtml), [elDiario.es](https://www.eldiario.es/politica/feijoo-abascal-asisten-primer-debate-tandem-llegar-moncloa-frente-sanchez-colau_1_13693175.html)
- The four candidates cleared their rally schedules on Thursday; Feijóo met self-employed associations ATA and CEAJE. [RTVE](https://www.rtve.es/noticias/20261126/diario-campana-29n/17309202.shtml)
- At a Madrid rally on Wednesday 25 November Feijóo said "ya no cuela el miedo" ("fear no longer works") and that the left "will not be able to add up with anyone"; the PP put attendance at about 10,000. [RTVE](https://www.rtve.es/noticias/20261125/feijoo-mitin-pp-madrid/17331125.shtml)

**Claimed or disputed**

- *El País*'s own poll average at the publication cut-off on Monday 23 November: PP 31.4%, PSOE 29.1%, Vox 17.9%, Frente Amplio and allied lists 6.8%; its model gives PP 129 seats, PSOE 116, Vox 61, and puts a PP–Vox absolute majority (176 seats) at 83–87% probability. These are estimates, not results (single source). [El País](https://elpais.com/espana/elecciones-generales/2026-11-26/quien-va-a-ganar-las-elecciones-la-probabilidad-de-cada-mayoria-segun-las-encuestas.html)
- Sánchez told *El País* a progressive majority is within reach "if we get to 75%" turnout; PSOE sources say post-debate trackings show a rise, which cannot be checked because trackings are unpublished. [El País](https://elpais.com/espana/2026-11-26/sanchez-estamos-tocando-con-la-yema-de-los-dedos-la-mayoria-progresista-si-llegamos-al-75-la-podemos-tener.html), [El Mundo](https://www.elmundo.es/elecciones/elecciones-generales/2026/11/25/6b070c74fdddff89748b456e.html)
- Deputy PM Yolanda Díaz called the PSOE's tactical-vote appeal a "catastrophe", arguing that finishing first "is worth nothing" without Frente Amplio seats. [Europa Press](https://www.europapress.es/nacional/noticia-diaz-ve-catastrofe-sanchez-pida-voto-util-psoe-primera-posicion-sirve-perder-gobierno-20261126140019.html)

**What next**: campaigning ends on Friday 27 November; Saturday is the day of reflection; voting is on Sunday 29 November.

### Supreme Court confirms voting ban for citizens naturalised under the "grandchildren law"

On Thursday 26 November the Tribunal Supremo (Supreme Court) rejected the government's last appeal against the precautionary suspension of voting rights for people naturalised under the Ley de Memoria Democrática's descendants provision ("ley de nietos"). The ruling lands amid opposition allegations about the integrity of the overseas vote, which the electoral authority has dismissed.

**Established facts**

- The court's administrative chamber upheld the exclusion of the 163,355 people on the list supplied by the government, whether registered abroad or in Spain; only the 901 who produced consular certification of descent from an exile, or others who do so before polling day, may vote. [Europa Press](https://www.europapress.es/nacional/noticia-supremo-defiende-no-ampliado-numero-afectados-voto-suspendido-ley-nietos-20261126154702.html), [elDiario.es](https://www.eldiario.es/politica/supremo-confirma-tres-dias-elecciones-163-000-nacionalizados-ley-nietos-no-podran-votar_1_13696267.html)
- The suspension originated in a petition by Iustitia Europa and Vox; the court restated it in an order notified on Friday 20 November. [elDiario.es](https://www.eldiario.es/politica/supremo-confirma-tres-dias-elecciones-163-000-nacionalizados-ley-nietos-no-podran-votar_1_13696267.html), [Tribunal Supremo](https://www.poderjudicial.es/cgpj/es/Poder-Judicial/Tribunal-Supremo/Noticias-Judiciales/El-Tribunal-Supremo-mantiene-la-exclusion-como-electores-de-las-personas-afectadas-por-las-cautelares-dictadas-en-relacion-con-la-llamada--Ley-de-Nietos-)
- The ruling criticises "errors or inaccuracies" in government data and covers 11,283 affected people resident in Spain (single source). [El Confidencial](https://www.elconfidencial.com/espana/2026-11-26/el-supremo-carga-contra-el-gobierno-por-sus_4473412/)
- The Junta Electoral Central (electoral board) unanimously rejected PP and Vox complaints about the overseas vote on Monday 23 November, saying no evidence had been provided (single source). [El País](https://elpais.com/espana/2026-11-25/la-junta-electoral-rechaza-por-unanimidad-las-quejas-de-pp-y-vox-sobre-noticias-sin-pruebas-contra-el-voto-cera.html)

**Claimed or disputed**

- The number affected is contested: the government told the court the true figure was 113,326 after removing minors and duplicates; the court kept the original 163,355. [El Confidencial](https://www.elconfidencial.com/espana/2026-11-26/el-supremo-carga-contra-el-gobierno-por-sus_4473412/)
- *La Vanguardia* quotes Feijóo as saying "it is confirmed there has been electoral fraud", referring to the ministerial instruction; Sánchez says PP and Vox are "questioning the cleanliness of the electoral system". [La Vanguardia](https://www.lavanguardia.com/politica/20261126/11693470/tension-electoral-avisos-sobre-posible-fraude-ultimo-cartucho-debate-cuatro.html), [El País](https://elpais.com/espana/2026-11-26/sanchez-estamos-tocando-con-la-yema-de-los-dedos-la-mayoria-progresista-si-llegamos-al-75-la-podemos-tener.html)
- *El Mundo* reports rising consular turnout in Mexico and Argentina and complaints of undelivered postal ballots in Europe, based on consular sources and voters. [El Mundo](https://www.elmundo.es/elecciones/elecciones-generales/2026/11/25/6b07109bfc6c837f368b45b2.html)

**Opinion and analysis**

- The conservative *ABC*'s editorial says nothing "permits the claim that electoral fraud exists" but accuses Sánchez of "sowing distrust" by suggesting the right may not accept the result. [ABC](https://www.abc.es/opinion/editorial-sanchez-siembra-desconfianza-urnas-20261124022930-nt.html)
- *La Vanguardia*'s campaign analysis argues the explicit fraud warnings from PP and Vox erode institutional trust and aim to mobilise their voters. [La Vanguardia](https://www.lavanguardia.com/politica/20261126/11693470/tension-electoral-avisos-sobre-posible-fraude-ultimo-cartucho-debate-cuatro.html)

**What next**: the overseas (CERA) count is expected around 3–4 December, according to *El Mundo*; the main case on the instruction remains undecided.

### Puigdemont amnestied and announces return on 12 December

**Established facts**

- On Monday 23 November the Supreme Court's criminal chamber declared the criminal liability of Carles Puigdemont, Antoni Comín and Lluís Puig extinguished under the amnesty law and closed the "procés" case against them. [Tribunal Supremo](https://www.poderjudicial.es/cgpj/es/Poder-Judicial/Tribunal-Supremo/Noticias-Judiciales/El-Tribunal-Supremo-declara-extinguida-la-responsabilidad-penal-de-Carles-Puigdemont-por-la-amnistia-en-la-causa-del--proces-), [La Vanguardia](https://www.lavanguardia.com/politica/20261125/11692596/puigdemont-confirma-regresara-catalunya-12-diciembre-acudira-parlament.html)
- In Brussels on Wednesday 25 November Puigdemont said he will fly to Barcelona on Saturday 12 December and go to the Catalan Parliament, "without asking forgiveness or permission"; he will give a lecture on independence strategy on 17 December. [El Mundo](https://www.elmundo.es/espana/2026/11/25/6b06b417fc6c83ba038b458e.html), [La Vanguardia](https://www.lavanguardia.com/politica/20261125/11692596/puigdemont-confirma-regresara-catalunya-12-diciembre-acudira-parlament.html)
- Catalan president Salvador Illa's office confirmed the two will meet (single source). [La Vanguardia](https://www.lavanguardia.com/politica/20261125/11692596/puigdemont-confirma-regresara-catalunya-12-diciembre-acudira-parlament.html)

**Opinion and analysis**

### Caretaker parliament ratifies five more decrees; Vox boycotts

**Established facts**

- The Diputación Permanente (the standing committee that acts for a dissolved Congress) ratified five decree-laws on Thursday 26 November: a 4.5% public-sector pay rise, an income-tax exemption for the minimum wage, workplace transparency rules, emissions-trading changes and R&D tax incentives. The PP backed three, opposed the emissions decree and abstained on R&D. [20minutos](https://www.20minutos.es/nacional/diputacion-permanente-convalida-sus-ultimos-cinco-decretos-antes-29n-entre-criticas-oposicion-ausencia-vox_7066199_0.html), [ABC](https://www.abc.es/espana/diputacion-permanente-congreso-convalida-cinco-decretos-sociales-20261126133138-nt.html)
- Vox stayed away, calling the session "electoral fraud" and vote-buying. [Europa Press](https://www.europapress.es/nacional/noticia-vox-no-acude-diputacion-permanente-acusa-sanchez-tratar-comprar-votos-tres-dias-29n-20261126100615.html)

**Claimed or disputed**

- Vox, citing media reports, says decrees approved since the election was called commit more than €30bn; the PP accused the government of "cara dura" (nerve); Sánchez says such measures have been passed "at every cabinet meeting" for eight years. [Europa Press](https://www.europapress.es/nacional/noticia-vox-no-acude-diputacion-permanente-acusa-sanchez-tratar-comprar-votos-tres-dias-29n-20261126100615.html), [20minutos](https://www.20minutos.es/nacional/diputacion-permanente-convalida-sus-ultimos-cinco-decretos-antes-29n-entre-criticas-oposicion-ausencia-vox_7066199_0.html), [El País](https://elpais.com/espana/2026-11-26/sanchez-estamos-tocando-con-la-yema-de-los-dedos-la-mayoria-progresista-si-llegamos-al-75-la-podemos-tener.html)

**What next**: four further decrees (transport aid, consumer-credit caps, the bank levy extension, bereavement leave) still await a vote, per *ABC*.

### Corruption cases: Ábalos release date set; new police report on Navarre tunnel

**Established facts**

- The Supreme Court set May 2042 as the end of former transport minister José Luis Ábalos's prison term and November 2040 for ex-adviser Koldo García, in the "mascarillas" (face-masks) case. [El Mundo](https://www.elmundo.es/espana/2026/11/26/6b08386ee85eced9618b456e.html), [El Confidencial](https://www.elconfidencial.com/espana/2026-11-26/tribunal-supremo-abalos-carcel-permisos-penitenciarios_4473333/)
- A Guardia Civil UCO report sent to Audiencia Nacional judge Ismael Moreno cites "possible irregularities" that could be criminal in the Belate tunnel contract awarded by Navarre's government to a consortium including Servinabar, a firm linked to former PSOE official Santos Cerdán. [Europa Press](https://www.europapress.es/nacional/noticia-uco-apunta-posibles-irregularidades-obra-tunel-belate-analizar-documentacion-navarra-20261126111252.html), [ABC](https://www.abc.es/espana/uco-apunta-posibles-irregularidades-adjudicacion-modificados-tuneles-20261126110120-nt.html)

**Claimed or disputed**

- The UCO's findings are investigators' suspicions, not judicial conclusions. Outlets give different contract values: €62.8m (Europa Press) and €76m including VAT (ABC). [Europa Press](https://www.europapress.es/nacional/noticia-uco-apunta-posibles-irregularidades-obra-tunel-belate-analizar-documentacion-navarra-20261126111252.html), [ABC](https://www.abc.es/espana/uco-apunta-posibles-irregularidades-adjudicacion-modificados-tuneles-20261126110120-nt.html)
