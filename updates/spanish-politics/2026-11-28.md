## Top stories

### Campaign ends: rival rallies in Madrid, Seville and Valencia

On Friday 27 November the parties held their last rallies before Saturday's *jornada de reflexión* (day of reflection), when campaigning is banned. Each camp used its closing event to show its strength and to set the terms of Sunday's vote. The PSOE turned the Thursday debate row over Feijóo's "calladita" remark into a direct appeal to women voters.

**Established facts**

- Pedro Sánchez closed the PSOE campaign at Madrid's Caja Mágica with Pedro Almodóvar and the band Los Planetas. He told the crowd that "la mayoría progresista la tenemos a la vista" ("the progressive majority is in sight") and again called for 75% turnout. [El País](https://elpais.com/espana/elecciones-generales/2026-11-27/sanchez-culmina-su-sexta-campana-con-el-mitin-mas-multitudinario-de-su-trayectoria-tenemos-la-mayoria-progresista-a-la-vista-si-se-puede.html), [ABC](https://www.abc.es/espana/sanchez-festival-cierre-campana-almodovar-batucada-planetas-20261127202951-nt.html)
- Sánchez recalled Feijóo telling Colau "¡Tú calladita!" and said "la España de 2026 no tiene calladitas a las mujeres". He named Nevenka Fernández and Ana Orantes, and warned that PP and Vox would repeal rights such as the euthanasia law, which Feijóo has said he wants to repeal. [El País](https://elpais.com/espana/elecciones-generales/2026-11-27/sanchez-culmina-su-sexta-campana-con-el-mitin-mas-multitudinario-de-su-trayectoria-tenemos-la-mayoria-progresista-a-la-vista-si-se-puede.html), [elDiario.es](https://www.eldiario.es/politica/sanchez-abarrota-caja-magica-madrid-campana-psoe-volver_1_13700400.html)
- In Seville, the only Andalusian province the PSOE won in 2023, Alberto Núñez Feijóo appealed directly to left-wing voters: "No os pido que dejéis de sentiros de izquierdas… La decencia no es de izquierdas o de derechas" ("I am not asking you to stop feeling left-wing… Decency is neither left nor right"). He was joined by Andalusian president Juanma Moreno. [La Vanguardia](https://www.lavanguardia.com/politica/20261127/11695380/feijoo-apela-votante-critico-psoe-ultimo-mitin-campana-sevilla-pido-cambieis-ideologia.html), [20minutos](https://www.20minutos.es/nacional/feijoo-pide-reeditar-cambio-andaluz-espana-con-voto-opcion-honesta-decencia-no-es-derechas-izquierdas_7066936_0.html), [RTVE](https://www.rtve.es/noticias/20261127/feijoo-mitin-cierre-pp-sevilla-elecciones-directo-cronica/17329585.shtml)
- Santiago Abascal closed Vox's campaign in Valencia's bullring. He called Sánchez "criminal" and "canalla", said he had "caused deaths" in the DANA floods and the Adamuz and Urda rail crashes, and told supporters that "puede haber cualquier tipo de trampa, cualquier tipo de intimidación, de violencia" ("there may be any kind of trick, intimidation or violence"). The crowd chanted an insult against Sánchez. [elDiario.es](https://www.eldiario.es/politica/abascal-acaba-campana-insultos-sanchez-asegura-elecciones-haber-trampas-violencia_1_13700156.html), [20minutos](https://www.20minutos.es/nacional/abascal-pide-votar-29-n-con-valor-cierre-campana-desde-valencia-advierte-que-las-urnas-no-se-llenan-con-encuestas_7066914_0.html)
- Ada Colau (Frente Amplio) closed in Villaverde, Madrid: "Los matones de la extrema derecha no nos van a intimidar, y no nos van a callar jamás" ("the far-right thugs will not intimidate us, and will never silence us"). Irene Montero, with Pablo Iglesias, closed for Podemos in Moratalaz and accused the PSOE of asking for the "voto útil" (tactical vote) and then doing "nada útil" (nothing useful). [RTVE](https://www.rtve.es/noticias/20261127/ada-colau-mitin-cierre-campana-frente-madrid-amplio-directo-cronica/17334130.shtml), [20minutos](https://www.20minutos.es/nacional/irene-montero-arropada-pablo-iglesias-reivindica-podemos-pura-valentia-hacer-frente-psoe-no-hace-nada-util_7066949_0.html)

- Frente Amplio's team says its goal is more than 10% of the vote and 18–20 seats, against polls of about 7%. [La Vanguardia](https://www.lavanguardia.com/politica/20261127/11695396/frente-amplio-exprime-exito-debate-colau-recuperar-impulso-calladita.html)

### Fraud claims and the "nietos" vote put pressure on the electoral machinery

**Established facts**

- The Junta Electoral Central (JEC, the Central Electoral Board) ordered polling stations and consulates to keep, unopened, the ballots of about 163,355 people naturalised under the *ley de nietos* (the "grandchildren's law", part of the Democratic Memory Law), whose right to vote the Supreme Court suspended. The ballots are to be recorded as "votos no escrutables" (uncountable votes). The 901 people who proved exile descent can vote. [La Vanguardia](https://www.lavanguardia.com/politica/20261127/11695085/junta-electoral-acuerda-mesas-electorales-conserven-votos-nietos.html), [RTVE](https://www.rtve.es/noticias/20261127/junta-electoral-voto-nietos-dni-electronico/17333736.shtml), [El Mundo](https://www.elmundo.es/espana/2026/11/27/6b09e625fc6c83cf3e8b458c.html)
- The JEC also ruled that if the QR check in the MiDNI app fails, only a physical ID document will be accepted. [RTVE](https://www.rtve.es/noticias/20261127/junta-electoral-voto-nietos-dni-electronico/17333736.shtml)
- Correos (the state postal service) accepted 1,012,033 postal votes: 8.86% more than in November 2019 and 59.05% fewer than in July 2023. The overseas register (CERA) reached a record of more than 2.66 million voters. [La Vanguardia](https://www.lavanguardia.com/politica/20261127/11695046/voto-correo-sube-8-8-respecto-2019-cae-60-respecto-2023.html), [ABC](https://www.abc.es/espana/voto-exterior-marca-record-266-millones-electores-20261127123733-nt.html)

**Claimed or disputed**

- The PP filed a complaint with the JEC alleging "graves incidencias" (serious incidents) in postal-vote delivery. It cited "hundreds" of its campaign mailings found in a Madrid rubbish container and asked for the case to go to prosecutors. Correos says it delivered 100% of the voting packs. The left-leaning *elDiario.es* says the PP is raising "the spectre of fraud" without evidence; the PP says the best defence against fraud is to vote for it "de forma masiva" (en masse). [La Vanguardia](https://www.lavanguardia.com/politica/20261127/11695068/pp-denuncia-jec-irregularidades-voto-correo-reparto-propaganda.html), [elDiario.es](https://www.eldiario.es/politica/pp-agita-fantasma-fraude-denuncia-junta-electoral-retrasos-voto-correo-sobres-propaganda-tirados-basura_1_13698792.html)
- RTVE's fact-checking unit rated as false claims circulating online that registering immigrants on local rolls (*empadronamiento*) lets them vote, or that dead people's census cards can be used to vote. [RTVE](https://www.rtve.es/noticias/20261127/elecciones-generales-2026-teorias-pruebas-pucherazo-29n/17333905.shtml)
- Unnamed officials told *El País* that an Interior Ministry cyber-security group has seen "hybrid" interference activity attributed to Russia, but no Iranian involvement. No data has been published (single source). [El País](https://elpais.com/espana/elecciones-generales/2026-11-28/blindaje-frente-a-la-desestabilizacion-rusa-y-el-riesgo-de-ciberataques-ante-el-29-n.html)

### Feijóo pulls out of TVE interview and files complaint against RTVE

**Established facts**

- On Friday morning Feijóo's team declined a 30-minute interview with Silvia Intxaurrondo on TVE's *La Hora de la 1*, after days of saying he would do it. He gave his last interviews to Cope and Esradio instead. Intxaurrondo said on air that the PP had "not even answered" her team's messages. The PP filed a complaint with the JEC over RTVE's alleged "falta de neutralidad" (lack of neutrality) (single source). [El País](https://elpais.com/espana/elecciones-generales/2026-11-27/feijoo-planta-a-tve-en-el-ultimo-momento-no-dara-entrevista-al-ente-publico.html)

### Courts: Ceuta witness, Adamuz reports, Zapatero's daughters

**Established facts**

- A protected witness, a Moroccan police informant, told Audiencia Nacional judge María Tardón that Tetuán police chief Mohamed El Oualidi told him on 10 July that "the final assault" on Ceuta was coming at the end of the month. [El Mundo](https://www.elmundo.es/espana/2026/11/27/6b097a1c21efa0f4398b458d.html), [ABC](https://www.abc.es/espana/confidente-declara-jefe-policia-tetuan-aviso-tres-20261127141932-nt.html)
- New reports sent to the Montoro judge investigating January's Adamuz crash, which killed 46 people, point to rail-infrastructure failures. A Guardia Civil report finds that sleepers, track and ballast at the crash site date from 1990 and that the second train went unnoticed for 47 minutes. The AITAT (the independent rail-accident investigator) found that Adif supervisors misapplied welding-safety procedures. [El Mundo](https://www.elmundo.es/andalucia/2026/11/27/6b084d93fc6c837b068b458b.html), [20minutos](https://www.20minutos.es/nacional/guardia-civil-confirma-que-tardo-47-minutos-conocer-existencia-un-segundo-tren-accidentado-adamuz_7066909_0.html), [El Confidencial](https://www.elconfidencial.com/espana/2026-11-28/investigacion-halla-soldaduras-adamuz-alerta-fallos-supervision_4472911/)
- Judge José Luis Calama will question former prime minister José Luis Rodríguez Zapatero's daughters, Laura and Alba, as suspects for alleged money laundering in the Plus Ultra case on Monday 30 November (single source). [20minutos](https://www.20minutos.es/nacional/las-hijas-zapatero-declararan-este-lunes-30-noviembre-como-imputadas-caso-plus-ultra_7066880_0.html)

**Opinion and analysis**

- The conservative *El Mundo* says the reports "shore up political responsibility" for Adamuz just hours before the vote. [El Mundo](https://www.elmundo.es/andalucia/2026/11/27/6b084d93fc6c837b068b458b.html)

**What next**: Polls open at 09:00 on Sunday 29 November and close at 20:00 (an hour earlier in the Canaries). The overseas (CERA) vote is counted from 4 to 7 December and could decide the last seats. [ABC](https://www.abc.es/espana/voto-exterior-marca-record-266-millones-electores-20261127123733-nt.html)

## Coverage notes

- Framing split along the usual lines. *elDiario.es* and *El País* stressed the PSOE's packed rally and the PP's unproven fraud claims. *El Mundo* and *ABC* stressed Adamuz, Ceuta and Vox's turnout in Valencia.
- Rally crowd figures are party claims. The *El País* report on Russian interference rests on anonymous sources.
- Europa Press pages again returned no article text. *ABC* and *El Confidencial* articles were partly paywalled, so only their opening paragraphs were used. Reuters, AP and EFE were not consulted directly.
- No polls can be published, and no campaign activity is allowed on Saturday 28 November, the day this update was written.
