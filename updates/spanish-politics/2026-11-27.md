## Top stories

### Final debate: a left-wing tandem, a PP–Vox truce and the "calladita" row

The four-way debate on the night of Thursday 26 November, the last set-piece before Sunday's general election, was very different from the Sánchez–Feijóo head-to-head of 19 November. Pedro Sánchez (PSOE) and Ada Colau (Frente Amplio) attacked together, while Alberto Núñez Feijóo (PP) and Santiago Abascal (Vox) left each other alone. A remark by Feijóo to Colau became the main talking point afterwards.

**Established facts**

- Colau opened by asking the others to condemn recent gender-violence killings. Sánchez and Feijóo did; Abascal refused to "follow any order". [RTVE](https://www.rtve.es/noticias/20261127/debate-electoral-cuatro-elecciones-generales-2026-cronica/17331240.shtml), [elDiario.es](https://www.eldiario.es/politica/sanchez-colau-lanzan-tandem-bulos-recortes-pp-vox-feijoo-abascal-ignoran_1_13697623.html)
- Colau asked how Feijóo could talk about good management when the PP keeps former Valencian president Carlos Mazón as a deputy with parliamentary immunity (*aforado*) after the DANA floods. Feijóo answered: "Buena gestión es ponerse al frente de una emergencia nacional y no salir huyendo de Paiporta y usted, calladita" ("…and you, keep quiet"). Sánchez called it "machismo". [elDiario.es](https://www.eldiario.es/politica/sanchez-colau-lanzan-tandem-bulos-recortes-pp-vox-feijoo-abascal-ignoran_1_13697623.html), [La Vanguardia](https://www.lavanguardia.com/politica/20261127/11694443/debate-trincheras-refuerza-division-izquierda-derecha-santiago.html)
- On leaving the studio Feijóo said "si ha molestado a alguien, por supuesto lo retiro" ("if it bothered anyone, of course I withdraw it"). Later, at PP headquarters, he said Sumar and the "Frente Estrecho" had stayed "calladitos y calladitas" ("silent") during "the most corrupt government". [El País](https://elpais.com/espana/elecciones-generales/2026-11-26/feijoo-salva-el-debate-en-medio-del-fuego-cruzado-contra-su-alianza-con-abascal.html), [RTVE](https://www.rtve.es/noticias/20261127/sanchez-feijoo-abascal-colau-reacciones-debate-calladita/17332954.shtml)
- Feijóo and Abascal barely spoke to each other. Their one difference came on the 2030 World Cup: Abascal said Spain "cannot co-organise any World Cup with Ceuta invaded". [El Confidencial](https://www.elconfidencial.com/espana/2026-11-27/abascal-ceuta-invadida-marruecos-1qrt_4473771/), [elDiario.es](https://www.eldiario.es/politica/sanchez-colau-lanzan-tandem-bulos-recortes-pp-vox-feijoo-abascal-ignoran_1_13697623.html)
- Colau told Sánchez that recent PSOE corruption cases involving people "of your utmost trust" needed more than removing them from their posts, and urged him to cut rents in a renewed coalition. [elDiario.es](https://www.eldiario.es/politica/sanchez-colau-lanzan-tandem-bulos-recortes-pp-vox-feijoo-abascal-ignoran_1_13697623.html), [La Vanguardia](https://www.lavanguardia.com/politica/20261127/11694443/debate-trincheras-refuerza-division-izquierda-derecha-santiago.html)

**Claimed or disputed**

- All four camps claimed victory. PSOE spokeswoman Rebeca Torró said "progressive Spain" had won; Pablo Bustinduy said Colau "dominated"; the PP's Borja Sémper expressed "much satisfaction"; Vox's Ignacio Garriga called the Sánchez–Colau partnership a "teatrillo" (a piece of theatre). [RTVE](https://www.rtve.es/noticias/20261127/sanchez-feijoo-abascal-colau-reacciones-debate-calladita/17332954.shtml)
- Feijóo's team told *El Mundo* that "our lead… remains intact". Internal PP data cited by *El País* say Vox has been losing support throughout the campaign. Neither claim can be checked, because polls can no longer be published. [El Mundo](https://www.elmundo.es/elecciones/elecciones-generales/2026/11/27/6b08b994e85eceb9638b456e.html), [El País](https://elpais.com/espana/elecciones-generales/2026-11-26/feijoo-salva-el-debate-en-medio-del-fuego-cruzado-contra-su-alianza-con-abascal.html)
- RTVE's fact-check found that Feijóo's figures of 76% higher house prices and 45% higher rents covered the period since 2018, not the last legislature. It found Sánchez's claim of 726,000 homes completed, "double" Rajoy's total, to be about 720,000, or 70% more. It rated a claim about Barcelona's rental supply "false". [RTVE](https://www.rtve.es/noticias/20261126/debate-cuatro-verificacion-datos-elecciones-generales-2026/17330993.shtml)

**Opinion and analysis**

- All seven of the centre-left *El País*'s analysts named Colau the winner, and RTVE's studio panel also leaned to Colau. Iván Redondo said the "calladita" was "pure, unconscious". [El País](https://elpais.com/espana/elecciones-generales/2026-11-26/quien-ha-ganado-el-debate-los-analistas-de-el-pais-se-decantan-por-ada-colau.html), [RTVE](https://www.rtve.es/noticias/20261127/quien-ganado-debate-cuatro-elecciones-2026/17333106.shtml)

Leyre Iglesias in *El Mundo* wrote that "Feijóo did well" against a "diabolical pincer", and Lucía Méndez, also in *El Mundo*, saw "neither loser nor winner". [ABC](https://www.abc.es/espana/feijoo-afianza-tras-debate-cuatro-20261126000519-nt.html), [ABC](https://www.abc.es/espana/sanchez-refugia-detras-colau-tratar-salir-vivo-20261126000344-nt.html), [El Mundo](https://www.elmundo.es/espana/2026/11/27/6b088283e85ece3c108b4594.html), [El Mundo](https://www.elmundo.es/elecciones/elecciones-generales/2026/11/27/6b088229e85ecea8388b4576.html)

### Feijóo pledges to resign if he does not become prime minister

**Established facts**

- Sánchez asked Feijóo whether he would recognise the result, citing PP and Vox claims about the census and the overseas vote. Feijóo "raised the bet": whoever does not form a government should resign, give up his seat and renounce his immunity. "Yo sí" ("I will"). Sánchez did not take up the offer. [elDiario.es](https://www.eldiario.es/politica/feijoo-promete-dimitira-si-no-gobierna-29n_1_13697643.html), [El País](https://elpais.com/espana/elecciones-generales/2026-11-26/feijoo-salva-el-debate-en-medio-del-fuego-cruzado-contra-su-alianza-con-abascal.html), [RTVE](https://www.rtve.es/noticias/20261127/debate-electoral-cuatro-elecciones-generales-2026-cronica/17331240.shtml)

**Claimed or disputed**

- PP sources told *elDiario.es* that the move was prepared in advance and called it "the bomb". Feijóo did not directly answer whether he would accept the result, though he said his offer was "the proof" that he would. [elDiario.es](https://www.eldiario.es/politica/feijoo-promete-dimitira-si-no-gobierna-29n_1_13697643.html)

### Ceuta returns to the centre of the campaign

Abascal made last July's mass entry of migrants into Ceuta his foreign-policy theme. Meanwhile, the Audiencia Nacional (National Court) investigation into how the government handled that crisis heard new witnesses.

**Established facts**

- Abascal told *ABC* that his first demand in government would be the "total and absolute recovery" of Ceuta (single source). [ABC](https://www.abc.es/espana/abascal-primera-medida-recuperacion-total-absoluta-ceuta-20261127015512-nt.html)
- Judge María Tardón asked Spain's consul in Tetuán, Fernando Villalonga, for the messages he sent the ambassador after the CNI (intelligence service) briefed him on the border. On Thursday 26 November the former chief of staff of the government's Ceuta delegation testified that the government's response was "cosmetic". The former delegate, Miguel Ángel Pérez Triano, is due to testify as a suspect next week (single source). [El Mundo](https://www.elmundo.es/espana/2026/11/26/6b082288fc6c831a088b4574.html), [El Mundo](https://www.elmundo.es/espana/2026/11/27/6b08975821efa07a2e8b4572.html)
- Police wiretaps in a Gijón court case show supporters of the neo-Nazi group Núcleo Nacional reporting attacks on Moroccans in Ceuta with baseball bats in early August (single source). [El País](https://elpais.com/espana/2026-11-27/simpatizantes-de-nucleo-nacional-acudieron-a-ceuta-para-agredir-a-inmigrantes-estamos-con-los-bates-de-beisbol-sacando-a-la-morralla.html)

**Claimed or disputed**

- The number of people who entered is disputed. Abascal said 100,000 people arrived in a city of 80,000, which *El Confidencial* notes he did not back up; *ABC* gives "close to 100,000"; *El País* says more than 72,000. [El Confidencial](https://www.elconfidencial.com/espana/2026-11-27/abascal-ceuta-invadida-marruecos-1qrt_4473771/), [ABC](https://www.abc.es/espana/abascal-primera-medida-recuperacion-total-absoluta-ceuta-20261127015512-nt.html), [El País](https://elpais.com/espana/2026-11-27/simpatizantes-de-nucleo-nacional-acudieron-a-ceuta-para-agredir-a-inmigrantes-estamos-con-los-bates-de-beisbol-sacando-a-la-morralla.html)

### Electoral board orders sanction proceedings against Ayuso

**Established facts**

- The Junta Electoral Central upheld a PSOE appeal and ordered Madrid's provincial electoral board to open sanction proceedings against regional president Isabel Díaz Ayuso. The case concerns a 3 November institutional video, recorded in her office, blaming Sánchez for the protest camp in the Puerta del Sol. The board said the video's breach of political neutrality was "beyond all doubt" (single source; Europa Press's report could not be opened). [elDiario.es](https://www.eldiario.es/politica/junta-electoral-central-ordena-abrir-expediente-sancionador-ayuso-video-pedro-sanchez-desalojo-sol_1_13697287.html)

**What next**: Friday 27 November is the last day of campaigning, followed by Saturday's day of reflection and voting on Sunday 29 November. Sánchez is calling for 75% turnout, which he says would give a progressive majority of 176 seats. [La Vanguardia](https://www.lavanguardia.com/politica/20261127/11694367/sanchez-movilizacion-historica-remontada-epica.html)
