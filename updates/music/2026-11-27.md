### TIDAL to remove and ban wholly AI-generated music

On Tuesday 24 November TIDAL announced that from 9 December it will reject tracks made entirely with generative AI and begin deleting those already on the service. Tracks made with the help of AI tools stay. This goes further than the tagging and demonetisation it brought in this summer, and further than Spotify, Apple Music or Deezer, which label or filter AI music but do not remove it.

**Established facts**

- Removals begin on 9 December. TIDAL's Robert Anderson wrote on X that this "will result in the deletion of 29 million songs" and the rejection of an estimated 80,000-85,000 songs a day. [Pitchfork](https://pitchfork.com/story/tidal-bans-ai-songs/), [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2326812/tidal-is-banning-ai-generated-music-entirely/), [DJ Mag](https://djmag.com/news/tidal-bans-ai-generated-music)
- TIDAL says 43% of tracks delivered in the last 90 days were detected as wholly AI-generated, and that 96% of AI-flagged tracks got no streams. [The Quietus](https://thequietus.com/news/tidal-to-delete-and-ban-fully-ai-generated-music/), [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/tidal-bans-ai-music-1235663794/)
- Detection is supplied by the French company Ircam Amplify. TIDAL says there is an appeals process for artists who think they were wrongly flagged. [Pitchfork](https://pitchfork.com/story/tidal-bans-ai-songs/), [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/tidal-bans-ai-music-1235663794/)

**Claimed or disputed**

- Some figures differ between reports. DJ Mag and Rolling Stone give 27-29 million tracks (about 6% of the catalogue). TIDAL says 65,000 tracks were removed for impersonating artists; Mixmag reports about 60,000. Mixmag spells the executive's name "Andersen". [DJ Mag](https://djmag.com/news/tidal-bans-ai-generated-music), [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/tidal-bans-ai-music-1235663794/), [Mixmag](https://mixmag.net/read/tidal-ban-ai-generated-track-entirely-december-news)
- TIDAL calls itself the first streaming service to do this. Pitchfork and Engadget point out that Bandcamp banned AI-generated music in January. [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2326812/tidal-is-banning-ai-generated-music-entirely/), [Pitchfork](https://pitchfork.com/story/tidal-bans-ai-songs/)
- Ircam Amplify claims 1% false positives. On that basis Pitchfork estimates about 290,000 genuine songs could be removed by mistake. A TIDAL spokesperson told Rolling Stone that most appeals so far had been "reaffirmed upon closer review". [Pitchfork](https://pitchfork.com/story/tidal-bans-ai-songs/), [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/tidal-bans-ai-music-1235663794/)

**Opinion and analysis**

- Engadget finds it "somewhat surprising" that TIDAL is first, since its owner Block is run by the AI enthusiast Jack Dorsey. [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2326812/tidal-is-banning-ai-generated-music-entirely/)

**What next**: Removals start on 9 December. Anderson says AI tracks will be gone by early 2027.

### Police link gas release at Don Toliver's Manchester show to jewellery thefts

An "unknown substance" was released during the US rapper's Co-op Live concert on Sunday 22 November. In the days since, police have suggested it may have been used to cover robberies, and fans have described being held for hours of decontamination.

**Established facts**

- Greater Manchester Police received nine reports of stolen jewellery at the concert and are looking into a link with the substance. No one has been arrested and the substance has not been identified. [NME](https://www.nme.com/news/music/substance-at-don-toliver-coop-live-show-could-be-linked-to-jewellery-thefts-police-say-3977049), [Stereogum](https://stereogum.com/2521553/manchester-police-say-don-toliver-concert-gas-incident-may-be-linked-to-jewelry-thefts/news/)
- Assistant Chief Constable Tom Harding said "similar robberies" had been reported at two of Toliver's European concerts. [NME](https://www.nme.com/news/music/substance-at-don-toliver-coop-live-show-could-be-linked-to-jewellery-thefts-police-say-3977049), [Stereogum](https://stereogum.com/2521553/manchester-police-say-don-toliver-concert-gas-incident-may-be-linked-to-jewelry-thefts/news/)
- One person was taken to hospital. GMP says 88 people were held at the Etihad Stadium while North West Ambulance Service decontaminated them "as a precaution". [BBC News](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cklywnlrwjexo)

**Claimed or disputed**

- One fan told the BBC he was "quarantined for six hours", made to change into paper overalls and threatened with arrest when he objected. GMP declined to comment. [BBC News](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cklywnlrwjexo)
- NME says the London shows moved to 14 December (Wembley Arena) and 15 December (The O2). Stereogum describes both as O2 dates. [NME](https://www.nme.com/news/music/substance-at-don-toliver-coop-live-show-could-be-linked-to-jewellery-thefts-police-say-3977049), [Stereogum](https://stereogum.com/2521553/manchester-police-say-don-toliver-concert-gas-incident-may-be-linked-to-jewelry-thefts/news/)

**What next**: Co-op Live has started a "full review". GMP says it will work with European police forces.

### Factory Floor return with *One Trip*

*One Trip* is the British electronic post-punk group's first album in a decade. It came out on Friday 27 November on Erol Alkan's Phantasy Sound. Factory Floor are now a trio: Gabe Gurnsey, Nik Colk Void and the new percussionist Joe Ward. [Bandcamp (label page)](https://factoryfloor.bandcamp.com/album/one-trip), [Stereogum](https://stereogum.com/2515714/factory-floor-announce-first-new-album-in-10-years-one-trip-hear-two-songs/music/)

**Opinion and analysis**

- In The Quietus's album of the week review, Robert Barry calls it "better than ever before" and "more techno-adjacent than ever". [The Quietus](https://thequietus.com/quietus-reviews/album-of-the-week/factory-floor-one-trip-review/)
- Clash is cooler (7/10). It finds the sound "impressively layered, if pretty familiar" and wonders how much it still sounds like Factory Floor. [Clash](https://www.clashmusic.com/reviews/factory-floor-one-trip)

### Clubs and venues under pressure

**Established facts**

- West Midlands mayor Richard Parker has promised to protect Digbeth venues, including XOYO Birmingham and Mama Roux's. More than 50 businesses there are threatened by the £1.75bn Midlands Rail Hub plans. [Mixmag](https://mixmag.net/read/west-midlands-mayor-promises-protect-under-threat-digbeth-venues-birmingham-news), [Birmingham Mail](https://www.birminghammail.co.uk/news/midlands-news/mayor-vows-fight-digbeths-venues-34936022)
- The Northern Ireland charity Free The Night is trying to raise £50,000 for its legal challenge to the Department for Communities over licensing reform, after being refused legal aid (single source). [Resident Advisor](https://ra.co/news/86484)
- Budapest's Atno club will close after a New Year's Eve party, blaming financial pressure on the underground scene (single source). The Welsh weekender Madruga says its fifth edition, on 23-25 July 2027, will be its last (single source). [Mixmag](https://mixmag.net/read/budapest-venue-atno-announces-imminent-closure-news), [Resident Advisor](https://ra.co/news/86487)

**Claimed or disputed**

- The owners of The Wyldes in Bude, Cornwall, are suing Live Nation Music UK in the High Court for about £1m. They say it promised eight concerts a year. Live Nation says it had a licence "but not an obligation", and blames the venue's remote location. [Mixmag](https://mixmag.net/read/live-nation-blames-undesirable-location-cornwall-venue-low-ticket-sales-news)
