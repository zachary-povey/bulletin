### Fallout from Kuwait attack: Gulf and EU condemn Iran, Tehran offers to investigate, Trump talks of victory

The diplomatic response to the drone attack that killed two Italian technicians in Kuwait on Thursday 26 November widened overnight. Arab governments and the EU blamed Iran. Iran's foreign minister denied involvement and offered to help find the source. Speaking to US troops on Thanksgiving, President Donald Trump said Iran still had a chance to make a deal, and named a site he would strike if the war resumed.

**Established facts**

- Italian Foreign Minister Antonio Tajani told the Senate that "drones from Iran" had targeted an air-defence station where two Rheinmetall Italia technicians were working. Kuwait's defence ministry said intercepted drones caused falling shrapnel that injured four people. [L'Orient Today](https://today.lorientlejour.com/article/1555747/kuwait-says-four-injured-after-iranian-drones-intercepted.html), [Iran International](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202611267797)
- European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen wrote that "we must step up pressure on Iran". [Naharnet](https://www.naharnet.com/stories/en/324051-eu-chief-urges-pressure-on-iran-after-italians-killed-in-drone-strike), [L'Orient Today](https://today.lorientlejour.com/article/1555747/kuwait-says-four-injured-after-iranian-drones-intercepted.html)
- On Thursday Trump told service members "we have victory at hand". He said the US was giving Tehran "every chance to make a deal", and that "if we do something, we will definitely hit Pickaxe Mountain". He announced no decision to strike, said he had spoken to Benjamin Netanyahu in recent days, and played down Russian and Chinese intelligence-sharing with Iran. [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/article-916297), [Al Jazeera/AFP](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/27/trump-downplays-russia-china-support-for-iran-during-talks-with-us-troops)

**Claimed or disputed**

- Iranian FM Abbas Araghchi called the incident "suspicious" and a "pre-cooked recipe for war". According to the opposition-aligned *Iran International*, he denied responsibility in calls with Kuwait's foreign minister and Tajani and offered to work with Kuwait to establish who launched the drones. The same outlet reports that Iran's embassy in Rome suggested a "false flag", and that the IRGC-affiliated Fars agency hinted at a Ukrainian role without evidence. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/11/27/iran-war-live-irans-araghchi-calls-deadly-kuwait-attack-suspicious), [Iran International](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202611267797)
- *Iran International* reports condemnations from Saudi Arabia, Bahrain, Qatar, the UAE and Jordan. It also reports that Kuwait invoked its right to self-defence under Article 51 of the UN Charter (single source). [Iran International](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202611267797)
- Trump said the US had fired 150 missiles to stop attacks on shipping in the Strait of Hormuz; he did not give a period, and the *Jerusalem Post* notes the figure is unverified. [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/article-916297)

**What next**: Italy said it would lodge a complaint with Iran's embassy, according to Iran International. The 11 December US funding deadline remains the next test of congressional limits on the war.

### Strike on Saudi Jazan refinery kills a worker; coalition says it hit the launch site

**Established facts**

- An aerial projectile hit the Jazan refinery on Thursday 26 November, killing a Nepali worker, injuring several others, starting a fire and briefly halting some operations, Saudi Arabia's energy ministry said. The ministry did not name the attacker; the Saudi-led coalition blamed the Houthis. [Al Jazeera/Reuters](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/26/attack-on-saudi-aramcos-jazan-refinery-kills-worker-disrupts-operations), [L'Orient Today](https://today.lorientlejour.com/article/1555781/attack-on-saudi-refinery-kills-one-triggers-temporary-shutdown.html)

**Claimed or disputed**

- Coalition spokesman Turki al-Maliki said its forces destroyed the Houthi ballistic-missile platform in Hodeidah used for the attack. Government forces claimed 767 strikes on Thursday that "neutralised" 117 Houthi fighters. The Houthis had made no immediate statement. None of these claims is verified. [Al Jazeera/Reuters](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/26/attack-on-saudi-aramcos-jazan-refinery-kills-worker-disrupts-operations)
- The government-run Saba agency said Houthi shelling near Taiz killed a woman and wounded seven; Houthi-affiliated media reported coalition air strikes on Sanaa. [Middle East Eye](https://www.middleeasteye.net/news/houthi-shelling-kills-one-woman-yemens-taiz)

**Opinion and analysis**

- Arab and Western officials told *Middle East Eye* that Riyadh intercepts most Houthi missiles and drones but has failed to protect energy sites, financial centres and airports. [Middle East Eye](https://www.middleeasteye.net/news/houthi-shelling-kills-one-woman-yemens-taiz)

### Gaza: Hamas weighs response to killings; UN data show a strained winter

**Established facts**

- Hamas leader Khalil al-Hayya said "the equation of continued killing and siege under an agreement is unacceptable" and that factions were discussing a response; he gave no details. Netanyahu and Defence Minister Israel Katz said "no one in the Hamas leadership will manage to evade us". [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/defense-news/article-916264)
- OCHA's report of 26 November says 405 aid trucks were offloaded at Kerem Shalom from 16 to 23 November, 6% fewer than the week before. It also reports 692 suspected meningitis cases since 1 October, mostly in infants. At least 600 aid workers have been killed since October 2023, 13 of them since the ceasefire. [OCHA](https://www.ochaopt.org/content/humanitarian-situation-report-26-november-2026)

**Claimed or disputed**

- Gaza's education ministry says Israeli strikes have killed 16 schoolchildren since 1 November. The IDF said it lacked information on two of the cases and that it takes measures to mitigate civilian harm. [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/26/gaza-ministry-schoolchildren-killed-this-month-israel-strikes)
- The ministry's post-ceasefire toll stood at 1,679 by 23 November, as relayed by OCHA. Tallies of Thursday's deaths still differ: "at least nine" (Reuters, via the *Guardian*) and 10 (hospital officials, via France 24). [OCHA](https://www.ochaopt.org/content/humanitarian-situation-report-26-november-2026), [France 24](https://www.france24.com/en/hamas-weighs-response-after-israel-kills-10-people-in-gaza)

### West Bank: settlers bulldoze family homes; rights group alleges sanctioned torture

**Established facts**

- Security cameras showed settlers using a bulldozer on two homes of the displaced Tubasi family in Jalud, near Nablus, early on Thursday 26 November. The family says a court order for their return has not been enforced. The Israeli military called it "a serious criminal act" and said it was investigating. [Al Jazeera/Reuters](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/26/israeli-settlers-damage-palestinian-familys-homes-in-west-bank-attack)
- OCHA recorded 55 settler attacks causing casualties or damage from 17 to 23 November and 79 demolitions in one week, including all 44 remaining structures in Khirbet Samra in the Jordan Valley. [OCHA](https://www.ochaopt.org/content/humanitarian-situation-report-26-november-2026)

**Claimed or disputed**

- A report by the Public Committee Against Torture in Israel (PCATI) on 42 complaints concludes that Israel's system "officially sanctions torture" to extract confessions. It cites official figures showing that more than 1,680 interrogation complaints since 2001 produced no indictment. The Israel Prison Service said prisoners are held "in accordance with the law"; the Shin Bet did not respond. [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/26/israel-officially-sanctions-torture-palestinian-prisoners-confessions-report), [Middle East Eye](https://www.middleeasteye.net/news/israeli-torture-produces-palestinian-confessions-courts-accept-report-finds)
