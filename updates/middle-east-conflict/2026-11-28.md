## Top stories

### Kuwait attack: Kuwait summons Iran's envoy, Italy says the drone was made in Iran

A day after the drone attack that killed two Italian Rheinmetall technicians, Kuwait made a formal protest to Iran and Italy said Kuwaiti investigators had traced the drone to Iran. Tehran still denies involvement and has offered to take part in an inquiry.

**Established facts**

- On Friday 27 November Kuwait's foreign ministry summoned Iran's ambassador, Mohammad Totonji. It gave him a note of protest over "continued Iranian attacks on the State of Kuwait", including Thursday's drone strike. [L'Orient Today/AFP](https://today.lorientlejour.com/article/1555856/kuwait-says-summoned-iran-ambassador-over-continued-iranian-attacks.html), [L'Orient Today](https://today.lorientlejour.com/article/1555870/evening-recap-aoun-salam-propose-extending-unifil-mission-as-kuwait-summons-iranian-ambassador-kuwait-summons-iranian-ambassador-khalil-al-hayyas-son-.html)
- Iran's foreign ministry said FM Abbas Araghchi told Antonio Tajani that Iran "had no role or involvement whatsoever" and was ready to work with Kuwait and Italy to "determine the origin of the incident". [L'Orient Today/AFP](https://today.lorientlejour.com/article/1555856/kuwait-says-summoned-iran-ambassador-over-continued-iranian-attacks.html)
- The Organisation of Islamic Cooperation's secretary-general condemned the strikes as "a flagrant violation of international laws and norms" (single source). [Iran International](https://www.iranintl.com/en/liveblog/202611214578)

**Claimed or disputed**

- Tajani says Kuwaiti investigations confirm the drone was made in Iran. He also says Tehran denied launching it from its territory and offered to join a commission of inquiry with Italy and Kuwait. Only the summary of Al Jazeera's live blog could be read; Kuwait's findings have not been published. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/11/28/iran-war-live-italy-says-drone-in-deadly-kuwait-attack-was-made-in-iran)
- Reports of the injured differ. Kuwait earlier said four people were hurt; AFP-based reporting in *L'Orient Today* names two wounded, a Pakistani and a Kuwaiti. [L'Orient Today](https://today.lorientlejour.com/article/1555870/evening-recap-aoun-salam-propose-extending-unifil-mission-as-kuwait-summons-iranian-ambassador-kuwait-summons-iranian-ambassador-khalil-al-hayyas-son-.html)

**What next**: Watch whether the joint inquiry Iran has offered goes ahead.

### Hormuz: US disables a ship leaving Bandar Abbas as the IRGC claims tankers hit mines

**Established facts**

- US Central Command said aircraft fired "precision munitions" at the stern of the Palau-flagged M/V *Pacific Archer* in the Gulf of Oman on Thursday 26 November, disabling its propulsion; the crew was unharmed. US officials say it had left Bandar Abbas. It is at least the 15th vessel the US has publicly said it disabled since the blockade began in April. [Al Jazeera/AP](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/27/us-military-says-it-disabled-merchant-ship-trying-to-breach-iran-blockade)
- IRGC aerospace commander Majid Mousavi wrote that Iran's enemies "should expect actions in the air and at sea". He called breaking the blockade Iran's "firm mission". [L'Orient Today](https://today.lorientlejour.com/article/1555872/enemy-should-expect-action-in-air-and-sea-irans-revolutionary-guard-aerospace-commander-warns.html)

**Claimed or disputed**

- The IRGC navy says two tankers using "unauthorised" routes hit mines near the Strait of Hormuz. Al Jazeera reports the IRGC as saying they caught fire near Oman's Khasab port; an earlier Al Jazeera report says the IRGC claimed one hit a mine and the other turned back. No independent confirmation has been found, and CENTCOM has disputed similar mine claims before. [Times of Israel/Reuters](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-november-28-2026/), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/27/us-military-says-it-disabled-merchant-ship-trying-to-breach-iran-blockade)
- CENTCOM rejected Iranian navy chief Shahram Irani's claim that Iran had struck the USS *Abraham Lincoln*. It said US forces and partners "do control the Strait of Hormuz" but there is "no toll for passing ships". [Iran International](https://www.iranintl.com/en/liveblog/202611214578)
- Iran's ambassador to Pakistan told CNN that messages are being exchanged with Washington through Qatar and Pakistan. [Iran International](https://www.iranintl.com/en/liveblog/202611214578)

### Gaza: strike on MSF clinic kills two guards; al-Hayya's son survives strike

**Established facts**

- MSF said two of its watchmen were killed late on Friday 27 November in an Israeli strike on its Gaza City clinic. It called it "a direct attack on MSF" on premises "clearly marked" and known to the army, and said 17 of its staff have now been killed in Gaza. WHO chief Tedros called the attack "outrageous and unacceptable". [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/28/israeli-strike-on-doctors-without-borders-clinic-in-gaza-kills-two-staff), [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/defense-news/article-916350)
- Hamas said Izzeldeen al-Hayya, son of its leader Khalil al-Hayya, survived a dawn strike on Friday with his family; relatives were wounded. Hamas linked the strike to threats by Israel Katz. [Al Jazeera/Reuters](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/27/israel-kills-four-in-gaza-as-hamas-chief-al-hayyas-son-survives-strike), [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/mena/2026/11/27/israel-says-it-killed-alleged-hamas-members-despite-gaza-ceasefire/)

**Claimed or disputed**

- The IDF says both men were Hamas members; it says one, Muayad Alizbach, was an artillery unit commander. It says they were planning attacks, and that it did not target medical infrastructure. MSF has repeatedly denied employing members of armed groups. The IDF gave no evidence and its claim is unverified. [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/defense-news/article-916350), [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-november-28-2026/)
- Wafa, the Palestinian news agency, reported four other Palestinians killed on Friday. The Gaza health ministry's post-ceasefire toll is 1,679, as cited by *The National*. Both figures come from Palestinian authorities. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/27/israel-kills-four-in-gaza-as-hamas-chief-al-hayyas-son-survives-strike), [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/mena/2026/11/27/israel-says-it-killed-alleged-hamas-members-despite-gaza-ceasefire/)
- Channel 12 reports the security cabinet will meet on Sunday 29 November on parts of the Trump plan, including facilities for the stabilisation force (single source). Board of Peace adviser Josh Gruenbaum told *Ynet* the board "will hand the keys" of Gaza to the UN if Israel keeps obstructing. [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/security-cabinet-said-set-to-discuss-advancing-stalled-trump-gaza-plan-amid-us-pressure/), [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/mena/2026/11/27/israel-says-it-killed-alleged-hamas-members-despite-gaza-ceasefire/)
- Israel's UN envoy Danny Danon demanded that UN aid chief Tom Fletcher correct his remarks on tents held up outside Gaza. Danon cites COGAT figures; the UN counts more than 150,000 tents stuck outside. The figures measure different things. [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/international/article-916335)

**Opinion and analysis**

- Former Israeli intelligence officer Danny Citrinowicz (INSS) told *The National* that Netanyahu "has never been genuinely committed" to the political parts of Trump's plan. [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/mena/2026/11/27/israel-says-it-killed-alleged-hamas-members-despite-gaza-ceasefire/)

**What next**: The cabinet meets on Sunday. Board of Peace envoy Nickolay Mladenov briefs the Security Council on Monday 30 November.
