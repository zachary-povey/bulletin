## Top stories

### Two Italians killed in drone attack on Kuwait; Iran denies responsibility

Two Italian defence-contractor technicians died on Thursday 26 November after a drone attack on a Kuwaiti air-defence site. Kuwait blames Iran; Iran's armed forces deny any operation. The deaths come amid a US naval blockade of Iran, a standoff in the Strait of Hormuz and stalled US-Iran talks, nine months after the war began with US-Israeli strikes in late February.

**Established facts**

- Kuwait's defence ministry and Italy's foreign ministry confirmed the two deaths; a Kuwaiti and a Pakistani were also wounded. The Italians worked for Rheinmetall Italia at an air-defence station. [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c39w9y5dx4k5o), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/26/two-italians-killed-after-iranian-drones-strike-facility-says-kuwait), [Al-Monitor](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/11/iranian-drone-attack-kuwait-leaves-two-italians-dead)
- The US Treasury on Monday 23 November sanctioned 22 entities and eight individuals it links to Iranian "shadow banking". [Iran International](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202611245272), [Middle East Eye](https://www.middleeasteye.net/news/qatar-needs-us-iran-resume-talks-end-war-pm-says)
- Qatar's prime minister, after meeting US Secretary of State Marco Rubio on 23 November, said the region could not tolerate renewed US-Iran attacks and that Qatar is pushing to revive talks (single source). [Middle East Eye](https://www.middleeasteye.net/news/qatar-needs-us-iran-resume-talks-end-war-pm-says)

**Claimed or disputed**

- Kuwait calls the attack "heinous Iranian aggression" and says the casualties were caused by debris from intercepted drones; Italy's ministry spoke of "an explosion caused by a drone attack". Iranian armed forces spokesman Abolfazl Shekarchi said "no military or offensive operations have been conducted" and, per Iranian state media, suggested a "false flag". Neither account is independently verified. [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c39w9y5dx4k5o), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/26/two-italians-killed-after-iranian-drones-strike-facility-says-kuwait)
- Iran's IRGC Navy claimed two tankers hit naval mines in the Strait of Hormuz early on Wednesday 25 November; The National says this is unverified and that maritime agencies have described unidentified projectiles in other recent incidents. [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/gulf/2026/11/25/trump-warns-of-war-funding-fight-as-tanker-attacks-raise-hormuz-tension/)
- Israeli diplomatic sources told Al-Monitor that Benjamin Netanyahu is pressing for action over an alleged Iranian chemical weapons programme and Washington is refusing; Tehran denies such a programme and Israel has published no evidence. [Al-Monitor](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/11/trump-resists-netanyahus-push-iran-military-action-over-alleged-chemical-weapons)

**Opinion and analysis**

- Andreas Krieg (King's College London) told Al Jazeera a Democratic push to bar funds for offensive operations is "potentially the first serious institutional constraint" on Trump, but would not itself end the war. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/26/can-trump-continue-with-iran-war-despite-funding-restrictions)
- An unnamed senior Arab official told the UAE-based *National* he expects "full-scale military confrontation" to resume. [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/mena/2026/11/26/arab-nations-fear-renewed-iran-war-as-officials-warn-of-far-reaching-impact/)

**What next**: US government funding expires on 11 December, the deadline Democrats hope to use; Trump told the New York Post the campaign is funded to the end of January.

### Israel kills three senior Hamas officials in Gaza in three days

Israel killed the head of Hamas's internal security service on Tuesday 24 November, his northern Gaza chief on Wednesday and a political official on Thursday, in what its leaders call a series of targeted killings. Gaza authorities say relatives and other people died too, and accuse Israel of trying to sow chaos despite the October 2025 ceasefire.

**Established facts**

- Naeem Abu Nahel was killed by a drone strike in Gaza City on 24 November; Mohammed Abu al-Ouf, his wife and two teenage daughters were killed in Shati camp on 25 November; Khaled Abu Nada, deputy education minister, and his wife were killed in Deir al-Balah on 26 November. Israel confirmed targeting all three men. [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c6rrg7ggq190o), [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cmd718zydz9qo), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/26/israeli-attacks-on-gaza-kill-senior-education-official-seven-other-people), [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/defense-news/article-916167)
- Netanyahu and Defence Minister Israel Katz said about 80 Hamas members had been killed in the past month. [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cqvg2n4mzl1jo), [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/defense-news/article-916167)

**Claimed or disputed**

- Thursday's toll differs: the BBC, citing Hamas-run Civil Defence, reports eight dead; Al Jazeera, citing health officials, at least 10. Neither is independently verified. [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cqvg2n4mzl1jo), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/26/israeli-attacks-on-gaza-kill-senior-education-official-seven-other-people)
- The IDF says Abu Nada ran Hamas's finances and directed funds to military build-up; Gaza officials describe him as an education official. Gaza's interior ministry says Israel is targeting police to "spread chaos". [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/defense-news/article-916167), [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cmd718zydz9qo)
- The Hamas-run health ministry counts at least 1,705 killed since the ceasefire, according to the BBC, which says the UN considers the ministry's figures reliable; a ministry spokesman told Al Jazeera "more than 1,600". Israel says six of its soldiers were killed in the same period. [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cqvg2n4mzl1jo), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/26/israeli-attacks-on-gaza-kill-senior-education-official-seven-other-people)
- Katz said on 26 November that Hamas must disarm or face an operation including "another evacuation of one million Gaza residents southward" (single source). [Naharnet](https://www.naharnet.com/stories/en/324028-israel-defense-minister-threatens-to-displace-1-million-gazans-if-hamas-won-t-disarm)

**Opinion and analysis**

- UN human rights chief Volker Türk warned on 25 November of a "widening risk" of atrocity crimes against Palestinians in Gaza and the West Bank, and reminded Hamas of its obligations to civilians. [UN News](https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/11/1168879)
- Unnamed Israeli security sources told the *Jerusalem Post* the Abu Nada killing is a "turning point" aimed at collapsing Hamas's civil control. [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/defense-news/article-916167)

**What next**: Hamas leader Khalil al-Hayya said factions would discuss a response, per Reuters as carried by Al Jazeera.

### Houthis strike Riyadh and Saudi oil and port sites; UK imposes sanctions

Yemen's Houthis hit Riyadh's financial district for the second time in a week and claimed attacks on the Ras Tanura refinery and Red Sea ports, in a war that reignited in July.

**Established facts**

- Saudi authorities said 11 people were wounded when a drone hit a tower in the King Abdullah Financial District on Tuesday 24 November. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/24/saudi-led-coalition-condemns-terrorist-attack-on-tower-in-riyadh), [DW](https://www.dw.com/en/houthis-claim-attacks-on-saudi-refinery-financial-district/a-80043632)
- The UK on 24 November froze the Houthis' assets and sanctioned four officials, citing attacks on Saudi Arabia and shipping and abuse of detainees. [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/uk-steps-up-response-to-renewed-houthi-aggression), [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/uk/2026/11/24/uk-sanctions-houthis-over-shipping-attacks-and-alleged-torture/)
- UN aid chief Tom Fletcher arrived in Yemen on 25 November, saying 364,000 people have fled since August and calling access to Taiz urgent. [UN News](https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/11/1168872)

**Claimed or disputed**

- The Houthis say they set Ras Tanura ablaze and damaged the ports of Jeddah and Jazan; the Saudi-led coalition says it downed four drones at Ras Tanura and acknowledges only minor damage at Jazan. Argus Media, cited by Middle East Eye, reported two hits at Ras Tanura. [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/gulf/2026/11/25/yemens-houthis-say-they-attacked-saudi-arabias-ras-tanura-refinery/), [Middle East Eye](https://www.middleeasteye.net/news/loud-booms-rock-riyadh-houthis-attack-saudi-ports)

**Opinion and analysis**

- Ahmed Nagi of the International Crisis Group told Al Jazeera the UK sanctions are mainly a political message. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/26/symbolic-why-did-uk-sanction-yemens-houthis-amid-attacks-on-saudi)
