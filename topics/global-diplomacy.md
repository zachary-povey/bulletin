## Scope

International relations and diplomacy worldwide: great-power relations (US, China, Russia,
EU, India), the war in Ukraine and its diplomacy, summits and multilateral bodies
(UN, G7, G20, NATO, BRICS, COP, WTO), treaties, sanctions and trade disputes, and
significant elections or changes of government that alter a country's foreign policy.

## Include

- Outcomes rather than photo opportunities: what was agreed, signed, sanctioned or broken off.
- Trade and tariff policy as an instrument of foreign policy.
- Emerging crises anywhere in the world (Africa, Asia, Latin America) that are drawing international response.

## Exclude

- Middle East conflict detail (covered by its own topic) unless it is part of a wider diplomatic story.
- UK and Spanish domestic politics (covered by their own topics).
- Ceremonial visits with no substantive outcome.

## Sources to favour

Reuters, AP, AFP, Financial Times, The Economist, BBC, Le Monde, Der Spiegel International, Politico
Europe, Foreign Policy, Nikkei Asia, South China Morning Post, The Hindu, Al Jazeera, Kyiv Independent,
official readouts and communiqués (compare each side's readout of the same meeting),
Chatham House, CFR, Crisis Group.
