## Scope

News from the parts of music the reader follows: post-punk and alternative rock; hip-hop
and grime; jungle, breaks and drum and bass; and alternative electronic music
(experimental, IDM and adjacent). UK scenes matter as much as US ones.

## Include

- Releases: album and EP announcements and release dates, notable singles, reissues and
  archival finds, and the critical reception of significant new records.
- Live: tour and festival line-up announcements (UK and Europe first), residencies, notable
  one-off shows and cancellations.
- Artists and bands: formations, reunions, splits, line-up changes, deaths and obituaries.
- Labels, clubs and venues: signings, label launches and closures, club and venue openings,
  closures and licensing fights, pirate and community radio.
- Scene and industry developments that affect this music: streaming and royalty changes,
  ticketing, touring costs and visas, funding for grassroots venues, sampling and
  copyright disputes, AI-generated music.
- Smaller and independent artists and labels, not only the best-known names.

## Exclude

- Mainstream chart pop, celebrity gossip, relationships and social-media feuds, unless there
  is a real consequence for the music (a cancelled tour, a court ruling).
- Awards-show fashion and red-carpet coverage.
- Rumoured releases and line-ups with no confirmation from the artist, label or promoter.
- Listicles, "best of" rankings and playlist round-ups (year-end lists may be mentioned once,
  briefly, in "In brief").
- Gear, equipment and production tutorials.
- Commercial EDM and mainstream dance-pop.

## Reporting notes

Give the practical details: release date and label for records, dates and cities for tours,
and where tickets or pre-orders are confirmed. Say which genre or scene an unfamiliar artist
belongs to in a few words. Reviews and scores are opinion: put them under "Opinion and
analysis" with the critic and outlet named, and give more than one view where critics
disagree. Announcements made by an artist or label are a primary source for what was
announced; link the announcement as well as the coverage where possible.

## Sources to favour

The Quietus, Pitchfork, Stereogum, The Guardian music, NME, The Line of Best Fit,
Loud and Quiet, Crack Magazine, Bandcamp Daily, The Wire, Resident Advisor, Mixmag, DJ Mag,
Inverted Audio, DUMMY, UKF, GRM Daily, The FADER, XXL, Complex, Clash, BBC News for industry
stories, and artists' and labels' own announcements.
