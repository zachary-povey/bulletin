## Scope

New trends in software and technology, and the technology economy.

## Include

- Software engineering: notable releases, languages, frameworks, infrastructure, developer tooling, open source, security incidents and significant vulnerabilities.
- AI: model and product releases, research results, compute and chips, regulation and safety.
- The tech economy: earnings of major tech companies, funding rounds and IPOs, M&A, layoffs and hiring, antitrust and regulation (US, EU, UK).
- Hardware and platforms where there is a genuine shift (semiconductors, cloud, devices).

## Exclude

- Gadget reviews, deals, and consumer buying guides.
- Rumour and leak coverage unless corroborated by multiple credible outlets.
- Crypto price movements (include crypto only for major regulatory or security events).
- Company PR presented as news with no independent confirmation.

## Sources to favour

Reuters, Bloomberg, Financial Times, The Verge, Ars Technica, TechCrunch, The Information,
Wired, MIT Technology Review, The Register, Stratechery, Hacker News front page (as a signal of what
engineers are discussing, then go to the primary source), company engineering blogs, release
notes, arXiv, SEC filings. Distinguish clearly between vendor claims (benchmarks,
performance numbers) and independently verified results.
