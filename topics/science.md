## Scope

Significant new findings and developments across the sciences: physics, astronomy and
space, biology and medicine, climate and earth science, chemistry and materials,
mathematics, and science policy and funding.

## Include

- Peer-reviewed results in major journals, and important preprints (flagged as not yet peer reviewed).
- Space missions and launches of scientific importance.
- Major clinical trial results and regulatory approvals.
- Science policy: funding, research integrity, major retractions.

## Exclude

- Single small studies hyped by press release, nutrition/lifestyle "studies show" stories.
- Health advice and wellness content.

## Reporting notes

For each finding give: what was actually measured or shown, sample size or scale where relevant,
where it was published, and what independent experts (not the authors) say. Link the paper itself
(DOI or journal page) as well as the coverage.

## Sources to favour

Nature News, Science (AAAS), New Scientist, Quanta Magazine, Scientific American, STAT News,
Ars Technica science, BBC Science, The Guardian science, Reuters, Phys.org (only as a pointer
to the paper), NASA/ESA, journal pages and arXiv/bioRxiv/medRxiv.
