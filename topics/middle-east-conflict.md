## Scope

Armed conflict and its diplomacy across the Middle East: Israel and the Palestinian
territories (Gaza, West Bank), Lebanon, Syria, Iran and its allies, Yemen and the Red Sea,
Iraq, and the roles of the US, Gulf states, Turkey, Egypt and the UN.

## Include

- Military developments, ceasefire and hostage/prisoner negotiations, humanitarian conditions.
- Diplomatic moves, UN Security Council and ICJ/ICC proceedings, sanctions.
- Domestic politics in the countries involved where it changes the course of the conflict.

## Exclude

- Graphic detail for its own sake; unverified social-media footage.
- Commentary with no new information.

## Reporting notes

This topic has heavy information warfare. Be especially strict:

- Attribute every casualty figure and battlefield claim to its source (e.g. "according to the
  Gaza health ministry", "the IDF said") and state whether it has been independently verified.
- Report each party's account of contested events side by side rather than choosing one.
- Use sources from different perspectives and regions and say where their accounts differ.

## Sources to favour

Reuters, AP, AFP, BBC, Financial Times, Al Jazeera, Times of Israel, Haaretz, The National (UAE),
Al-Monitor, Middle East Eye, L'Orient Today, UN OCHA and other UN agency reports, ICRC,
International Crisis Group, ISW (for military mapping, noting its perspective).
