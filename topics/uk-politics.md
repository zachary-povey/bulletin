## Scope

Politics and government of the United Kingdom: Westminster (government, opposition,
Parliament, legislation, by-elections, party leadership and polling), major policy
(economy and the Budget, NHS, immigration, housing, energy, defence, education),
the devolved governments in Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland, and the UK's
relations with the EU and other countries where they are driven by domestic politics.

## Include

- Decisions, votes, announcements and data releases (ONS, OBR, Bank of England) with real policy consequences.
- Significant polling shifts and electoral events.
- Court rulings and inquiries that bear on government.

## Exclude

- Sport, celebrity and royal-family colour stories.
- Crime stories with no policy angle.
- Westminster gossip and personality rows unless they affect who holds power or what gets passed.

## Sources to favour

BBC News, Financial Times, The Guardian, The Times, The Telegraph, Sky News, Reuters,
Politico London Playbook, Institute for Government, IFS, Hansard, GOV.UK and ONS primary releases.
Balance left- and right-leaning outlets when reporting opinion.
