## Scope

National politics of Spain: the government and Cortes Generales, the main parties
(PSOE, PP, Vox, Sumar, Podemos, Junts, ERC, PNV, EH Bildu), budgets and legislation,
judicial cases with political weight, regional politics where nationally significant
(Catalonia, Basque Country, Madrid, Andalusia, Valencia), elections and polling (CIS and
private pollsters), and Spain's position within the EU.

## Include

- Read Spanish-language sources directly; write the update in English.
- Give Spanish names of institutions and laws on first mention with a short English gloss.
- Economic news (employment, housing, inflation) when politically salient.

## Exclude

- Sport, celebrity, royal-family lifestyle coverage.
- Purely local stories with no national significance.

## Sources to favour

El País, El Mundo, La Vanguardia, elDiario.es, El Confidencial, ABC, RTVE, EFE, Europa Press,
Reuters, Politico Europe, the BOE and Moncloa/Congreso primary sources. Spanish media is
strongly aligned politically: always pair outlets from different camps when reporting contested stories.
